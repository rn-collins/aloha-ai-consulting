04Ask a generic AI the one question that matters. Watch it fail.+

The Operator Execution Gap · live

Large language models fail most confidently exactly where an operator's stakes are highest. Same question, two answers: the fluent-but-wrong generic reply, and the primary-source-grounded one. The distance between them is what a wrong assumption costs.

✕ Generic LLM · unsourced

"Is marijuana Schedule III now — are state dispensaries federally legal?"

"Yes — marijuana was rescheduled to Schedule III, so cannabis is now federally recognized as medicine and state-legal dispensaries are in the clear."

Confident. Fluent. Wrong on three counts — and wrong in the exact way that gets a tax position, a funder memo, or a filing rejected.

✓ Aloha AI · primary-source grounded CFR · Fed. Reg.

Same question, answered from the record:

No broad move to Schedule III has taken effect. The new merits hearing ran from June 29 through July 15, 2026; transcript corrections and post-hearing briefing remain pending, with briefs due August 17, 2026. A completed hearing is not a final rule, and marijuana remains in Schedule I unless and until a final rule changes the schedule.

If broad rescheduling is finalized, it still would not make state adult-use dispensaries federally legal: Schedule III substances remain subject to federal prescription, registration, manufacturing, and distribution controls. Any effect on §280E, research, or a particular cannabis business depends on the final rule and the operation's facts.

Confidence: High on procedural status as of 2026-07-31. Basis: 91 FR 22777 and the DEA tribunal order governing transcript corrections and post-hearing briefs. Recheck the live docket before use.

OEG, the Operator Execution Gap, is the distance between what AI says and what a regulated operator can act on. Closing it is the entire job.

08A repeatable intelligence method, not a one-off scrape+

How it works

Every Aloha AI monitor runs the same three-stage pipeline. In a production build, that is what keeps the facts right next month, not just today.

Regulatory Intelligence Layer (RIL)

Primary sources only (Federal Register, CFR, dockets, agency orders), captured with source and retrieval date. No headline aggregation.

Resolve to an Operator Layer (ROL)

Raw law becomes a decision an operator can act on: what changes, for whom, with what confidence, closing the Operator Execution Gap.

Auto-refresh & alert

In a production build, the monitor re-checks its primary sources on a schedule (Upstash · Vercel · Exa) and can ping you when a docket opens or a rule shifts, so it never goes stale.
