The governing terms

Open the section you need.

6 documented sections. Named Aloha AI resources link directly to their canonical pages.

01Report a possible error privately+

Use the current Microsoft Bookings link, choose any available time, and write “correction report — no meeting needed” in the note. Include the affected route or file, the exact statement or behavior, what you believe is wrong, the source or evidence supporting the report, when you observed it, and a safe reply address. Do not include credentials, confidential client material, or sensitive personal information. RN will treat the booking as a report rather than a meeting.

02Response states+

A report moves through received, triage, investigating, confirmed or not-substantiated, correcting, monitoring, and closed states. Urgent safety, privacy, security, legal-status, eligibility, price, access, and materially misleading capability issues are prioritized. Acknowledgment or investigation is not an admission that the report is correct.

03What a confirmed correction must record+

The durable ledger records a stable correction identifier, report and decision dates, owner, category and severity, prior and corrected text or behavior, supporting authority, root cause, every known affected public output, propagation decisions, tests or controls added, response state, closure evidence, and any notification limitation. Superseded wording remains in the ledger rather than being silently erased.

04Affected-output analysis+

Review is not limited to the page named in a report. Canonical source, generated routes, search and feed records, structured APIs, downloads, duplicated copy, linked tools, and known downstream publications are checked when relevant. Each is corrected, bounded, withdrawn, or documented as unaffected.

05Closure and notification+

A confirmed correction closes only after the corrected source is committed, affected checked-in outputs are rebuilt or explicitly dispositioned, relevant tests pass, and production evidence is recorded when the output is public. Direct notification is attempted when the affected recipient is known and the consequence warrants it. Public-site publication does not prove that copied, downloaded, cached, indexed, or third-party versions were updated.

06Current public ledger and limits+

The machine-readable public ledger is available at /api/corrections.json. It includes confirmed site corrections reconstructed from committed evidence and future confirmed corrections governed by this policy. It is not a complete history of every ordinary edit, stylistic revision, unpublished draft change, client correction, or error that has not been reported or discovered. This process does not guarantee an error-free site.

Accountability record

Evidence, method, assumptions, and limits.

The public record includes the conditions that limit how it should be read.

Evidence

  • A public private-reporting route with a no-meeting instruction
  • A versioned machine-readable ledger preserving superseded and corrected states
  • A release-blocking evaluator for required fields, affected-output analysis, response state, and closure evidence

Method

  1. Preserve the reported and superseded state before correction
  2. Trace canonical and derived outputs before deciding scope
  3. Correct, qualify, suspend, or withdraw according to consequence
  4. Close only with commit, test, output-disposition, and production evidence appropriate to scope

Assumptions

  • The deployed site matches the evaluated repository tree
  • The Microsoft Bookings path remains available for private correction reports

Limitations

  • The ledger cannot establish that every error has been discovered
  • Copied, cached, downloaded, indexed, or third-party versions may persist
  • Client-confidential corrections are not published in the public ledger
