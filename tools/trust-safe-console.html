011. Orchestration — Harvey is one node, not the system+

Firms are not buying a single model. They are wiring several drafting and research tools into their workflow. Without a governing layer, each one can put an unverified authority in front of a court. The Trust-Safe Operating Layer sits between those tools and the filing/review system.

Example stack shown for illustration. The two AI research assistants are CoCounsel Legal (grounded in Westlaw / Thomson Reuters) and Lexis+ with Protégé (grounded in LexisNexis); Harvey's own research alliance is with LexisNexis. The layer is tool-agnostic and maps to whatever the firm actually runs.

Harvey is one node, not the system. The governance and evaluation layer is the product.

Example MCP tool contract

In production the layer runs as a Model Context Protocol server between the drafting assistant and the filing system:

tool: verify_citations input: { draft_text, jurisdiction, approved_sources[] } output: { risk_score, // 0-100 citations: [ { text, status, // verified_primary | unverified | flagged_fabrication reason, source_checked } ], gate: "blocked_until_attorney_signoff" }

Pre-filing verification checklist

Every box must be cleared by the responsible attorney before this draft is cleared for filing. The tool assists; the lawyer certifies.

  • I personally opened and read each cited authority from a primary source.
  • I confirmed every citation exists and the quoted holding matches the actual text.
  • I resolved or removed every item flagged as a possible fabrication.
  • No client-confidential information was entered into a non-approved AI tool (Rule 1.6).

Methodology note (illustrative)

  • Sample set: 240 gold-standard tasks per run, graded against attorney-authored answer keys across 6 jurisdictions.
  • Hallucination rate: share of outputs containing at least one citation or holding with no matching authority.
  • Jurisdiction accuracy: share of tasks that apply the controlling jurisdiction's rule correctly.
  • Citation pass rate: share of cited authorities that clear structural and existence checks.
  • The human baseline is a licensed attorney working the same task set. The delta pill shows each run's gap to that baseline.
  • Numbers here are fabricated for demonstration and do not describe any real product or firm.

055. Adoption — where 2026 pilots actually fail+

The technology rarely fails; adoption does. Trust is a neurological state: repeated safe exposure lowers threat response and builds the habit loop. Set an estimated adoption rate per stage to see where your funnel leaks.

Three change-management levers

  • Safe exposure: low-stakes, reversible first tasks so the threat response never fires. Trust is built by repetition, not by a training deck.
  • Visible governance: when lawyers can see the verification gate and audit trail, the tool feels like a safeguard rather than a liability — converting exposure into trust.
  • Peer advocacy: a respected internal champion who demonstrates wins moves habitual users to advocates faster than any top-down mandate.

Where 2026 pilots actually fail: the drop-off between trust and habitual use, not the model quality.

Demonstration artifact. Not legal advice. Funnel figures are your own estimates, computed in your browser.
