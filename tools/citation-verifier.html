01A hallucinated case name is a filing you can't take back+

Why this exists

Since 2023, courts have sanctioned lawyers for filing AI-fabricated citations. A fluent draft can still invent a citation, reporter, year, holding, or quotation. ABA Formal Opinion 512 explains how existing professional duties apply when lawyers use generative AI. Separately, Rule 11 requires an attorney who presents a filing to certify that its legal contentions follow a reasonable inquiry. This instrument is only an initial structural screen; it cannot discharge either responsibility.

What it flags

Invented reporter series (there is no F.4d or F. Supp. 5th), years that can't exist (a published opinion dated in the future), and malformed volumes or pages. These are the fingerprints a fabricated cite usually leaves.

What it can't do alone

A well-formed citation can still point to no real opinion, cite overruled law, or fail to support the sentence. This public engine checks structure only. A licensed attorney must open an authoritative source and verify existence, status, quotation, and proposition support before filing.

03Deterministic checks now, deeper verification in a commissioned workflow+

How it works

The public layer is a fast structural screen that runs on your device and needs no network. A commissioned workflow can add approved research sources, human review, and a record of what was checked.

Parse every citation

The engine reads US reporter citations out of the draft: volume, reporter, page, and year, plus the case name where it can find one. This runs entirely in your browser.

Check the structure

Each cite is tested against a known-reporter allow-list and series ceilings, so an invented series like F.4d or F. Supp. 5th is caught, along with impossible years and malformed volumes or pages. A defect this clear marks a likely fabrication.

Score and gate

The public result is a review list; it does not certify a filing or enforce a pre-filing block. In a firm deployment, the workflow can remain blocked until a named attorney records verification of each authority.

Add source verification

A firm version can query an approved case-law source through a private backend and route the result to a licensed attorney. The integration must be tested against the source's current authentication, coverage, licensing, and citation behavior.

Evidence

Method

  1. Capture the claim
  2. Inspect the cited source
  3. Classify support and conflict
  4. Record reviewer judgment

Assumptions

  • The cited source can be accessed

Limitations

  • The tool does not determine legal truth or replace expert review
  • Passing structure does not establish that an authority exists, remains good law, is quoted accurately, or supports a proposition
