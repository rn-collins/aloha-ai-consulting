01Triage is the slow, repetitive part of tracking legislation+

Why this exists

A compliance or policy team watching a legislature reads dozens of bills to find the handful that may create real obligations. Certain review candidates use recurring terms such as "shall," "must," "prohibited," and "penalty." This tool performs that narrow lexical first pass. It flags configured terms; it does not identify every obligation or decide what any provision means.

What it surfaces

Six visible lexical categories: candidate obligations, prohibitions, deadlines, thresholds, public authority, and enforcement language. Each match is paired with a review question. The result reports configured terms only; it does not infer stakeholders, quote clauses, assign risk, or summarize the instrument.

What it will not do

It does not read for meaning, weigh legislative intent, resolve ambiguity, or tell you whether an obligation applies to your client. Pattern matching finds candidate language. A licensed attorney reads the source and decides. The questions-for-counsel list is built for exactly that handoff.

03Pattern matching, in a fixed order+

How it works

Every run applies the same deterministic passes to the same text, so the same input always returns the same map. No model, no randomness, no network.

Tag the issues

The text is scanned against six published lexicons: obligations, prohibitions, deadlines, thresholds, public authority, and enforcement.

Leave actors to the reviewer

The engine does not infer who is covered or who carries a duty. The category question directs the reviewer back to the complete clause, definitions, exceptions, and cross-references.

List the matched terms

The result lists only the configured terms that matched. It does not extract, quote, highlight, or paraphrase the surrounding sentence.

Abstain or hand off

If no configured term matches, the tool abstains without clearing the text. If terms match, fixed category questions frame the handoff. No risk level, summary, legal conclusion, or compliance result is produced.
