The problem

Serious organizations hit the same three walls with AI.

They cannot trust the facts

Generic AI fails on the exact details that carry consequences: the citation, quota, statutory trigger, deadline, eligibility rule, or source date.

Their people do not adopt it

A rollout stalls when nobody designs the trust, habits, permissions, and review points around the tool.

The system has no operating owner

A demo can impress without surviving. Durable AI needs ownership, maintenance, governance, evaluation, and a path into actual work.

The operating gap

The Operator Execution Gap is the distance between what AI says and what you can safely act on.

Aloha AI closes that gap through visible evidence, explicit decision logic, bounded model roles, human accountability, and workflows built around the institution that must use them.

The standard

A credible answer needs a visible basis.

For evidence-heavy work, fluency is not proof. Aloha AI designs for provenance, versioning, uncertainty, review, and responsibility.
