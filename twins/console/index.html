Decision contract

Deterministic checks run before any model judgment: injection, unsupported claims, PII, scope, consent, and human-review requirements.

Sentence-level provenance

The safe draft is decomposed into claims. Each claim points to its supporting artifact or to a human-authored connective phrase.

Why this matters

A reviewer can distinguish sourced facts from generated connective language, inspect whether a claim is supported, and reject a draft without guessing how it was produced.

What approval would do in production

Write an append-only audit event, preserve the approved text and evidence snapshot, pass the message to a deterministic delivery adapter, and update CRM state. This public lab performs none of those network actions.
