A six-minute self-screen for anyone running an AI twin, avatar, or synthetic-voice agent. Twenty questions across the six places these deployments actually get people into trouble. You answer, your browser scores it, and you get a plain-language read on where you stand.

Not legal advice. This is an educational screening tool, not counsel. It is built by a JD candidate, not a licensed attorney, and it does not create an attorney–client relationship. 01What the screen examines+
  • Consent and likeness: written, use-scoped consent for every cloned voice or likeness, including third parties and deceased people.
  • AI disclosure: clear disclosure at first contact and for realistic synthetic replicas.
  • Provenance and labeling: machine-readable synthetic-media marking, platform labels, and end-to-end verification that credentials survive upload.
  • Substantiation of claims: evidence held before health, efficacy, outcome, AI-capability, review, or endorsement claims ship.
  • Platform and channel: LinkedIn automation boundaries and CAN-SPAM requirements for honest headers, a physical address, and a working opt-out.
  • Sector overlay: licensed-field boundaries, responsible-professional identification, and enforced hand-offs for legal or medical advice.
02Where this goes next: the Twin Readiness & Compliance Audit+

This screen shows you the shape of the risk. The audit closes it: a structured walk-through of consent records, disclosure, provenance, claim substantiation, channel hygiene, and sector rules for your specific deployment, with a remediation punch-list you can hand to your team or your counsel.
