About RN Collins and Aloha AI

I work on questions too messy for a single discipline.

My work moves through law, neuroscience, developmental psychology, research, education, AI systems, emerging industries, and product development. The fields change. The recurring task does not: understand how knowledge and decisions move through a complex environment, make the hidden structure explicit, and build a system people can actually use.

The through-line

From how people understand the world to how institutions operationalize it.

Psychology taught me to look at development, context, behavior, and meaning. Neuroscience and anatomy trained me to move between mechanisms, evidence, and education. Research operations taught me that rigor depends on procedures, source control, roles, and records. Law makes authority, interpretation, consequence, and accountability unavoidable. AI makes it possible to turn more of that structure into usable infrastructure, but only when the underlying reasoning remains visible.

Aloha AI is the commercial and product expression of that body of work. It is not limited to one industry or one interface. It focuses on complex work where sources matter, rules and exceptions matter, adoption matters, and a human remains responsible.

Formation

Each field added a different part of the method.

Psychology and development

People are not interchangeable users.

Developmental psychology, learning, identity, family, culture, motivation, and context shape whether a system is understandable, trustworthy, and usable.

Neuroscience, anatomy, and medical education

Mechanism and explanation must meet.

Technical accuracy is insufficient when knowledge cannot be taught, transferred, assessed, or used by the intended person.

Research and regulatory operations

Rigor is operational.

Source hierarchies, protocols, approvals, records, coding systems, revision ledgers, and completion gates turn good intentions into reproducible work.

Law and legal engineering

Authority, exceptions, and accountability cannot be hidden.

Legal work sharpens the distinction between retrieval, interpretation, judgment, advice, approval, and the consequences of getting them wrong.

AI systems and product development

The interface is only the surface.

The durable value lies in the knowledge model, rules, workflows, evidence, human review, governance, maintenance, and commercial structure underneath.
