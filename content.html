Content and adoption systems

Make knowledge usable without flattening the expertise behind it.

Aloha AI builds the research, voice, editorial, review, training, distribution, and measurement system around AI-supported communication. The goal is not more output. It is a repeatable process that preserves accuracy, judgment, authorship, trust, and institutional memory.

The real problem

Content quality and AI adoption fail for the same reason: the operating system is missing.

A prompt cannot decide which sources deserve trust, how expert nuance should survive, what claims require verification, who approves publication, or how staff learn to use the workflow consistently.

Research is disconnected from production

Sources, notes, interviews, claims, drafts, and corrections live in separate places with no traceable path into publication.

Voice is treated as a style preset

A few adjectives cannot preserve reasoning, evidence habits, sentence logic, taboo patterns, audience assumptions, and editorial judgment.

Training ends at tool demonstration

Staff see features but lack approved use cases, review standards, examples, escalation rules, and a supported way to practice.

System layers

Six layers from source to learning.

Evidence

Research architecture

Source hierarchy, claim register, retrieval dates, interviews, citation rules, and unresolved questions.

Identity

Voice and editorial system

Reasoning patterns, vocabulary, evidence expectations, prohibited habits, examples, and decision rules.

Production

Workflow and templates

Intake, briefs, drafting, review, approvals, repurposing, versioning, and handoffs.

Control

Claims and risk review

Fact checking, legal or scientific review points, disclosures, permissions, rights, and corrections.

Adoption

Training and support

Role-based learning, practice tasks, rubrics, office hours, examples, and usage standards.

Learning

Measurement and improvement

Quality, cycle time, adoption, corrections, audience response, platform behavior, and workflow revisions.

Deliverables

Build the parts your team will actually operate.

Research-to-publication OS

Source and claim registers, briefs, drafting stages, review gates, approvals, correction records, and archive structure.

Voice and editorial specification

A working standard grounded in real samples, editorial decisions, prohibited patterns, and annotated examples.

AI-use playbook

Approved tasks, prohibited uses, data rules, prompt patterns, verification, human review, and escalation.

Content operations system

Calendar, intake, assignment, workflow status, repurposing, distribution, ownership, and maintenance.

Training program

Role-based modules, demonstrations, practice tasks, assessment, facilitation guidance, and reinforcement.

Distribution diagnostic

Evidence-based review of policy constraints, discoverability, format, audience response, and platform distribution without pretending hidden causation is known.

Platform and suppression claims

Observed distribution problems are investigated, not declared.

What can be assessed

Policy restrictions, content eligibility, indexing, discoverability, account status, audience behavior, format, posting patterns, rights issues, and documented platform signals.

What cannot be known from the outside

A platform’s complete ranking logic, undisclosed internal actions, or a single causal explanation for reach. Findings should carry confidence and alternative explanations.
