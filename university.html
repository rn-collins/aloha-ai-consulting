What this is

Aloha AI University is open education for owners, operators, and teams who want to use generative AI well. It answers three questions: what these tools do, where they fall short, and how to build a workflow around them that holds up. Learning stays free, with no paywall and no email gate. When you want a partner to build the system, that path is here too.

06Grounded, honest, and on your sideOpenClose

Why this exists

Adoption is a trust problem before it is a tools problem.

Most AI training sells a feeling. This program teaches the mechanics: how the models work, where they break, and how to verify what they give you. We teach from primary sources and stay candid about limits, because a team that trusts its tools for the right reasons is a team that actually uses them. That is the whole point.
