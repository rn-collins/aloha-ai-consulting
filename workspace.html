05Organization and first project+

The intended authenticated workspace would begin with an organization and a first project. Public sign-in, account creation, and workspace provisioning are not available from this page.

  • Overview: see the organization, active project, and current workspace state.
  • Drafts: persist work in progress against the correct organization and project.
  • Evidence: save titled source material and keep it attached to the work it supports.
  • Review: inspect the intermediate work, preserve approval behavior, and keep accountable judgment in the loop.
06Persist drafts and evidence+

A draft belongs to an organization and project rather than an ungoverned chat. Evidence is stored with its title and source text so the workspace can keep claims, supporting material, and review together.

The public resource registry describes what each system does. The authenticated workspace carries that context into operational work, where access, persistence, review, and approval matter.

07Access and availability+

Workspace access is provided through defined engagements, pilots, programs, or licensed implementations. Feature availability varies by engagement and maturity, and workspace output does not replace accountable professional judgment.

Method

  2. Collect bounded inputs
  3. Generate inspectable intermediate work
  4. Require review and approval

Assumptions

  • Sensitive work is configured for the relevant access and retention requirements

Limitations
