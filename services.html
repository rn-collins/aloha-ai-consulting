The choices above are enough to begin. These four records show what the work includes and how responsibility stays visible.

Aloha AI studies the decisions, evidence, rules, workflows, people, and risks behind your work, then designs and builds the research systems, decision tools, governed AI workflows, and operating infrastructure it actually requires.

01A systems practice for evidence-heavy, judgment-sensitive work.+

What this is

The work combines research architecture, workflow design, governance, product thinking, implementation, and human adoption. The goal is to make recurring expert work more reliable, inspectable, and usable.
