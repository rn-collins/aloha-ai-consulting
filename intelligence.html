Regulatory and market intelligence

Know what changed, what it means, and what still requires judgment.

Aloha AI turns changing legal, regulatory, scientific, market, opportunity, and institutional source material into verified, dated, decision-ready intelligence. The system records where each claim came from, when it was checked, what remains unresolved, and who is responsible for acting on it.

The difference

Research answers a question once. Intelligence keeps the answer usable.

A serious intelligence system must define sources, authority, verification, status, change detection, interpretation, distribution, ownership, and maintenance. Without those elements, a dashboard or briefing can become stale while still looking current.

One-time research

A bounded answer for a defined question and date, with citations, limitations, and unresolved issues.

Maintained intelligence

A source register, monitoring cadence, change log, alert thresholds, and repeated review for a decision that remains active.

Decision infrastructure

Intelligence connected to owners, workflows, rules, approvals, and downstream actions rather than delivered as information alone.

Source hierarchy

Authority is defined before collection begins.

ClassExamplesUseLimit
Operative primary sourcesStatutes, regulations, official orders, filed court documents, agency dockets, official program rulesControlling requirements and procedural statusMay require interpretation by licensed or accountable professionals
Authoritative recordsOfficial datasets, registries, clinical-trial records, government reports, institutional policiesVerified facts, dates, status, and structured dataCoverage and update lag must be stated
Peer-reviewed and technical sourcesJournal articles, standards, technical documentationEvidence, methods, mechanisms, and technical contextQuality, applicability, and disagreement must be assessed
Secondary contextReputable journalism, analysis, commentary, company materialsDiscovery, stakeholder perspective, narrative contextNot substituted for operative authority when primary sources exist

The operating workflow

Every finding moves through a visible chain.

1

Discover

Locate candidate sources and define the universe.

2

Verify

Confirm identity, authority, date, applicability, and completeness.

3

Structure

Convert findings into fields, statuses, relationships, and evidence records.

4

Interpret

Explain consequence, uncertainty, conflict, and required professional judgment.

5

Maintain

Recheck sources, log changes, notify owners, and retire stale conclusions.

Deliverables

Choose the output the decision requires.

Decision brief

A concise, sourced answer with issue framing, findings, implications, uncertainties, and recommended next decisions.

Source and evidence register

Canonical sources, authority class, retrieval dates, effective dates, verification status, and notes.

Monitor and change log

Defined queries, cadence, last checked date, detected changes, alert thresholds, and owner.

Entity or opportunity dossier

Verified organization, program, person, product, jurisdiction, eligibility, relationship, and source fields.

Comparison matrix

Normalized criteria, source-backed values, unresolved conflicts, confidence, and decision relevance.

Decision packet

The evidence and operational artifacts required for leadership, counsel, a funder, regulator, or internal reviewer.
