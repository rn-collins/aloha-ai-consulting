Behind the wall

How to read the work without overreading it.

The proof wall is the quick way in. Open the notes below when you want the taxonomy, evidence rules, private-work boundaries, or ways a build can become a paid engagement.

This collection organizes Aloha AI’s public tools, reference demonstrations, research infrastructure, educational products, and selected engagement work by system type and operational status. Each public item should make its purpose, evidence, limits, maintenance, and current availability clear.

01Four system families make the range legible.OpenClose

Canonical taxonomy

01 · Research

Research and intelligence systems

Discover, verify, structure, compare, monitor, and maintain legal, regulatory, scientific, market, opportunity, or institutional information.

02 · Decisions

Decision and assessment systems

Adaptive intake, eligibility, scoring, classification, matching, compliance review, and guided decision support with explicit rules and exceptions.

03 · Operations

Knowledge and workflow systems

Agents, procedural copilots, document workflows, approval pipelines, internal knowledge systems, and governed operating structures.

04 · Products

Educational and AI-enabled products

Courses, complete programs, templates, diagnostics, client portals, specialized search, licensing assets, and maintained knowledge products.

02A deployment count is not a quality or production claim.OpenClose

Status language

Usable now

The route performs its stated function publicly. Data freshness and limitations are shown on the item itself.

Owned and reviewed

A named owner checks defined sources, logic, or content on a stated cadence and records changes.

Dated proof of method

The system demonstrates architecture or reasoning but is not represented as continuously current or production-ready.

Described without exposure

The problem, system type, role, and deliverable can be explained while protecting client, participant, source, or proprietary information.

05Every future build entry will answer the same questions.OpenClose

Required case-study record

Problem and user

What recurring work existed, who performed it, what failed, and who needed the output.

System architecture

Sources, data model, rules, AI role, deterministic logic, workflow, human judgment, governance, and maintenance.

Evidence and status

What was tested, what result is known, what remains uncertain, current availability, last review, and commercial status.

Licensing

License a framework, curriculum, dataset, tool, template estate, or maintained knowledge product under defined rights.

Partnership

Co-develop, fund, distribute, validate, or commercialize a larger system with agreed governance and ownership.

Commission a new system where the problem is sufficiently recurring, consequential, and structured to justify infrastructure.
