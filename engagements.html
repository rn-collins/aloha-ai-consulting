Biopharma · psychedelics

Regulatory intelligence for a psychedelics-biopharma company

Leadership needed the post-Executive-Order scheduling landscape translated into commercial timelines it could actually plan against.

What was delivered

  • A primary-source regulatory-intelligence memorandum: the EO's operative provisions, the CSA § 811(j) 90-day interim mechanism, the CNPV-versus-statutory-PRV distinction, APA constraints on DEA action, and the cannabis-rescheduling timeline as a cautionary base rate.
  • A per-company commercial-timeline analysis across four public psychedelics developers.
  • A scoped Regulatory Intelligence Layer to keep the picture current as rules move.
Deliverables produced · engagement scoping
Nonprofit · psychedelic-health media

An in-house AI content system for a founder-led health institute

A founder needed to scale trustworthy content and stand up an in-house AI capability without losing her voice or her compliance footing.

What was delivered

  • A three-part strategy package: a platform-suppression brief tracing the legal architecture from Section 230 through the EU Digital Services Act; a GLP-1 and psychedelics therapeutic-convergence brief; and a distribution-and-AI-strategy memo covering tool stack, prompt-library structure, client pipeline, and nonprofit-tax (UBIT) compliance.
  • A 13-week AI content-system build plan: voice-profile capture, platform-intelligence infrastructure across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and Substack, and recurring deep-research dossiers.
Strategy + build plan delivered
Nonprofit · end-of-life care

A 48-hour AI and funder-strategy sprint for an end-of-life-care nonprofit

A small research nonprofit needed a fundable evidence architecture and a working AI knowledge system, fast.

What was delivered

  • Five client-facing strategy documents within roughly 48 hours: an LLM/closed-GPT scoping proposal with a five-layer data architecture and legal-disclaimer architecture, a peer-expert research briefing, a tiered funder landscape across two distinct funding lanes, and a state Medicaid evidence-architecture memo.
  • A deployed, working LLM knowledge-platform prototype — five-layer data architecture, an intelligence retrieval layer, natural-language query, and lead capture — built as a funder proof-of-concept.
