## Decision boundary

The review establishes what the local repository supports. It does not certify production deployment, external integrations, ongoing maintenance, validated performance, commercial capacity, or responsive rendering. A browser-local control may be recorded as working when the generated page contains the applicable control and repository tests cover the local behavior; that decision does not convert the object into a production system.

Content-like objects may be marked complete as published content records. This is not a final word-by-word substantive certification and does not close the later individual-product completion or R10 release-review obligations.
