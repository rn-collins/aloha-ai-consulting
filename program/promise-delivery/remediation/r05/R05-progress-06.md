## Curriculum contract

1. Define tracked and material.
2. Build the source and record workspace.
3. Bound the watch list.
4. Collect without claiming completeness.
5. Detect and classify change.
6. Summarize from the source.
7. Score and route with reasons.
8. Review, release, and recover.
9. Test, document, and decide.

The learner produces a dated coverage contract, authority hierarchy, source register, canonical versioned records, collection receipts, classified change events, source-grounded alerts, an inspectable relevance rubric, review queues, an evaluation set, incident and correction controls, and a bounded release decision. Unknowns, collection gaps, source failures, ambiguous legal status, and missing human approval remain blocking.
