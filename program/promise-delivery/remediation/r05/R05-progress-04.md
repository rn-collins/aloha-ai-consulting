## 1. Purpose

This unit converts “Build a Governed AI Operator” from a ten-module curriculum preview into a complete canonical open course and learner experience. The course teaches a design, sandbox, evidence, and self-review method. It does not provide a connected operator, production credentials, security certification, enrollment, grading, or a credential.

## 2. Canonical curriculum delivered

- 10 modules
- 20 substantive lesson records and generated lesson routes
- 11 feedback-bearing assessment contracts
- 5 authoritative NIST and OWASP source records
- 1 capstone project
- 2 weighted rubrics
- 10 outcome mappings
- 1 planned tool record with an explicit non-operational boundary
- 1 explicit non-issued credential record

The modules cover mandate and consequential actions; workspace and sandbox; operator state; immutable approval; tool authority; deletable context; injection and control; evaluation; one bounded first job; and change-controlled operation.

## 3. Learner evidence package

The course requires an operator mandate, consequential-action policy, data and secret-name registers, sandbox specification, inspectable state machine, immutable approval-packet contract, tool contracts, permission and context registers, faithfulness review, injection and incident evidence, a representative evaluation set, a synthetic first-job run, a deliberately blocked run, and a bounded go/no-go decision.

Critical controls include:

- consequential execution remains default-denied;
- approval attaches to an exact destination, payload, tool, evidence, risk, expiry, and content hash;
- editing, expiry, replay, or reviewer mismatch voids approval;
- preparation adapters do not hold consequential execution authority;
- retrieved content is untrusted data, not policy or instruction;
- uncertainty, abstention, escalation, stop, revocation, rollback, incident, and audit states remain explicit;
- evaluation measures approval bypass, unauthorized execution, false support, missed escalation, recovery, and reviewer burden;
- course completion is not production authorization.

## 4. Delivery and privacy boundaries

Open lessons and browser-local self-records are available. Enrollment, submissions, instructor review, tutor access, grading, certificates, badges, identity verification, account-synced progress, credential issuance, connected accounts, and production deployment are unavailable.

Learners are instructed to use synthetic or non-sensitive examples and not to place confidential, personal, privileged, regulated, payment, authentication, or safety-critical information into a course exercise.
