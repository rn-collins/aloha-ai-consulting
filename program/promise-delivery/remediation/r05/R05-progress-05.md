## Curriculum contract

1. Define readiness before retrieval.
2. Inventory sources and owners.
3. Map questions, coverage, and gaps.
4. Govern freshness and lifecycle.
5. Prepare structure and metadata.
6. Expose duplicates and contradictions.
7. Apply access and sensitive-data controls.
8. Evaluate retrieval and answers.
9. Issue a bounded readiness decision.

The course keeps authority, coverage, freshness, structure, conflict, privacy/access, evaluation, and release separate. Unknown and unresolved states remain visible. Critical access, conflict, or evaluation failures cannot be concealed by an aggregate readiness score.
