### Purchase and artifact availability

Six nonexistent Gumroad acquisition statements are replaced with an explicit no-checkout/no-purchase/no-license/no-download state. Each artifact remains in the governed build queue.

The route now states that it is a dated educational architecture, not a current compliance kit. Operational reliance requires verification against current Oregon authority and qualified Oregon counsel.

### Citation Verifier and Trust-Safe console

The research note no longer claims that Citation Verifier retrieves or checks real sources. It states the implemented structural-parser boundary. The console is described as a browser-local interface/logic demonstration, not proof of connected practice-wide orchestration.

### Continuing education

Unverified submission-readiness, active accreditation/co-sponsorship progress, credit, and notification-list claims are removed. The canonical record now describes research targets and states that no provider application, co-sponsorship, accreditation, enrollment, notification list, or credit is available.

### Workspace

Workspace is reclassified from beta product language to research architecture. The canonical record states that no public sign-in, account creation, provisioning, or production access is available.

### Course previews

The six incomplete catalog courses are reclassified from beta to research. Their existing enrollment-closed, tutor-planned, credential-unavailable, and curriculum-preview boundaries remain. The complete open Citation Verifier materials retain their separate bounded delivery contract.

### Commercial contact

The Northeastern-owned email action is removed from the public contact contract. Written intake is disabled until a brand-owned channel exists, and the page warns visitors not to send confidential, privileged, health, or sensitive material through the current public site.

### Creator Content System

The system is reclassified from Production to Research and described as a documented method/proposed delivery architecture rather than a public product runtime or connected production system.
