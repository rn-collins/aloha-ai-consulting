# Dependency-ordered roadmap

## Gate 0 — Frozen baseline

Generate and review the route inventory, canonical-resource inventory, interactive occurrence inventory, promise ledger, dashboard, and freeze hashes. Broader feature work is blocked until all counts reconcile.

## Tranche 1 — Truth and conversion

Correct materially inaccurate availability, access, enrollment, credential, privacy, live-data, legal, and download language. Repair misleading enabled controls and broken commercial conversion paths.

## Tranche 2 — Existing asset connection

Match every represented build, tool, course, template, source, repository, deployment, and service to a canonical destination. Surface assets that already exist and document unresolved ownership or access.

## Tranche 3 — Proof-of-concept completion

Complete the most prominent thin tools and course experiences. Priority order is based on public prominence, visitor harm, strategic value, shared dependency leverage, and effort—not visual appeal.

## Tranche 4 — Shared product architecture

Build reusable status contracts, direct downloads, examples, version/last-tested metadata, tool runtime contracts, evaluation records, search/link graph controls, and only then any justified authentication, persistence, storage, submissions, grading, administration, payments, or credential issuance.

## Tranche 5 — Substantive expansion

Build remaining courses, lessons, assessments, projects, templates, playbooks, monitors, tools, and build documentation to their acceptance criteria.

## Tranche 6 — Governance and clean-room closure

Make promise checking release-blocking, reconcile every ledger record to a terminal state, rerun the entire inventory independently, and require zero unrecorded signatures before closure.

## Initial prioritization rules

P0: inaccurate or harmful claim; enabled action that falsely implies delivery; legal/privacy/credential/payment/access risk.

P1: prominent course, tool, build, workspace, download, or conversion promise with absent or thin delivery.

P2: valuable connection, documentation, depth, or shared architecture gap.

P3: low-prominence editorial or discovery improvement without present visitor harm.
