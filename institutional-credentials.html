The governing terms

Open the section you need.

6 documented sections. Named Aloha AI resources link directly to their canonical pages.

01Exact-claim rule+

A degree, enrollment, role, publication, fellowship, license, certification, membership, or institutional affiliation is published only at the level recorded in the register. Similar labels are not interchangeable: enrolled is not graduated; contributed to is not employed by; published by is not peer reviewed; used a vendor tool is not endorsed or certified by that vendor.

02Evidence classes+

Person-specific institutional records, an institution's public directory or verification, official program information, and independent public corroboration answer different questions. A program page can establish that a program exists; it does not establish that a named person enrolled or graduated. A third-party biography can corroborate a claim; it is not an institution-issued record. Private records are not published merely to make a public claim more persuasive.

03Current and historical status+

Current enrollment, employment, appointment, license, and membership claims require a status date and a recheck trigger. Historical experience is described as historical. Expected graduation years are projections, not awarded credentials. No site language may turn a candidate status into a completed degree or a past contribution into a current institutional appointment.

04No endorsement or institutional voice+

Naming a school, publisher, employer, client, vendor, or program identifies the bounded relationship only. It does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, approval, authorization to speak for the institution, or institutional review of Aloha AI. Marks and names remain subject to the Rights and Attribution policy.

05Aloha AI learning records+

Aloha AI University completion marks, downloads, assessments, portfolios, and browser-local records are self-records unless a resource expressly states otherwise. They are not enrollment, grades, academic credit, continuing-education credit, certificates, badges, licenses, or independently verified credentials.

06Register, review, and correction+

The machine-readable register at /api/institutional-credentials-register.json records selected public claim families and their evidence boundaries. It is not a complete résumé or background investigation. A status change, conflicting source, stale review, correction report, or copy change reopens the claim. Possible errors may be reported through the Corrections route.

Accountability record

Evidence, method, assumptions, and limits.

The public record includes the conditions that limit how it should be read.

Evidence

  • A selected machine-readable claim register
  • Person-specific, program-existence, and corroboration evidence classes kept distinct
  • A release-blocking inventory and exact-claim evaluator

Method

  1. Inventory public credential and affiliation language
  2. Normalize variants to a bounded exact claim
  3. Record evidence class, status, date, owner, and change trigger
  4. Remove or qualify language broader than the evidence
  5. Re-evaluate every public-copy change

Assumptions

  • Public corroboration pages accurately reproduce their publisher's current text
  • The deployed site matches the evaluated repository tree

Limitations

  • Private institutional records are not exposed in this repository
  • The register does not independently verify identity, enrollment, graduation, employment, licensure, publication review method, or current institutional standing
  • External pages can change after retrieval
