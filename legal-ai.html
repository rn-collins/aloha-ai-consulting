The design problem

Fast output is not the same as supervised legal work.

A defensible workflow must distinguish what the model generated, what authority was retrieved, what each authority actually supports, what remains uncertain, which information may be exposed, and which human approved the result.

Authority risk

A citation may be fabricated, outdated, nonbinding, distinguishable, or unrelated to the proposition it appears to support.

Confidentiality and privilege

Inputs, tool settings, retention, integrations, access, and vendor contracts can change the risk profile.

Supervision and competence

A legal team needs defined review standards, training, escalation, and accountability rather than informal individual use.

Process opacity

An answer can look polished while hiding missing sources, unresolved conflicts, assumptions, and incomplete review.

The workflow contract

Every AI-supported legal task needs explicit boundaries.

Mission

Defined task and user

The precise outcome, intended user, authorized matter, jurisdiction, and stage of work.

Inputs

Approved data and sources

What the system may receive, where material may be processed, and which authority classes it must retrieve.

Model role

Bounded assistance

Retrieval, extraction, comparison, drafting, classification, or organization, with prohibited actions stated.

Evidence

Support requirements

Citation, quotation, pinpoint, source date, court or agency, procedural posture, and proposition-level support.

Human review

Named approval gates

Who reviews facts, authority, analysis, confidentiality, filing readiness, client advice, and final use.

Operation

Logging and maintenance

Versioning, incident response, correction, evaluation, retention, ownership, and change control.

Verification gates

The system cannot advance merely because text was produced.

Gate 1

Source existence

Confirm that each authority exists and the retrieved document is the correct record.

Gate 2

Authority and status

Confirm jurisdiction, court, agency, date, precedential status, amendments, history, and current validity.

Gate 3

Proposition support

Read the source and verify that it supports the exact proposition, qualification, and scope.

Gate 4

Lawyer judgment

Counsel evaluates relevance, conflict, strategy, professional duties, client context, and final use.

Example task allocation

Use deterministic rules, retrieval, generation, and human judgment for different jobs.
