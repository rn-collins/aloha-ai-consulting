The standard

The same discipline behind the rest of Aloha AI applies here: nothing is generated loosely, and every claim traces to its source.

  • Primary-source citations, verified against the original research.
  • A four-cycle correction and QA pass on every course.
  • Timed agendas, measurable learning outcomes, and an evaluation form per course.
  • Ethics courses aligned to NASW, ACA, APA, and ABA standards.
  • Neuroscience-grounded, pan-disciplinary framing.

Accountability record

Evidence, method, assumptions, and limits.

A product claim is only as trustworthy as the conditions attached to it.

Evidence

  • Primary scientific, legal, regulatory, ethical, and professional sources
  • Applicable board and accreditor requirements
  • Learning objectives, timed agendas, assessments, and evaluation records

Method

  1. Define the practitioner and measurable learning outcome
  2. Build from primary and authoritative sources
  3. Audit claims and citations across four correction cycles
  4. Align ethics content to the applicable professional standards
  5. Confirm accreditation before representing credit as available

Assumptions

  • Accreditation and co-sponsorship requirements will be verified for each audience and jurisdiction
  • Qualified presenters and reviewers will be engaged where required

Limitations

  • No accreditation application or co-sponsorship arrangement is represented as submitted, pending, or approved; no credit is available
  • Educational content is not legal, medical, clinical, tax, or other professional advice
