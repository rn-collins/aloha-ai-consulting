# Editorial review checklist

- [ ] Each factual proposition maps to a current, dated source or is labeled inference.
- [ ] Neuroscience language preserves population, task, timing, uncertainty, and causal limits.
- [ ] Platform statements include platform, first-party URL, retrieval date, and status.
- [ ] Text, visual, title, and CTA describe the same topic.
- [ ] The audience can inspect evidence, understand uncertainty, and decline the action.
- [ ] The action is proportionate, reversible, and assigned to a human owner.
- [ ] Accessibility, rights, sponsorship, privacy, and professional boundaries were reviewed.
- [ ] Reviewer recorded approve, revise, reject, or escalate.
