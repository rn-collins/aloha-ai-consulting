# EDSA quick-reference card

**E — Evidence:** atomic claim · authority · date · scope · uncertainty

**D — Discussion:** context · alternatives · limitations · what changes the answer

**S — Self-Determination:** audience fit · disclosure · pause/exit · no coercion

**A — Action:** one proportionate step · owner · stop condition · review point

**Dual appraisal:** audience intelligibility and choice are not the same as platform eligibility and ranking.

**Hard stop:** diagnosis · causal neuroscience promise · guaranteed trust/reach/outcome · hidden interest/uncertainty · fear/shame/false urgency.

EDSA is an editorial decision architecture. It cannot diagnose a nervous system, predict an individual's response, establish scientific causation, guarantee trust, guarantee platform distribution, or replace legal, medical, scientific, accessibility, or platform-policy review.
