# Three worked before-and-after rewrites

## 1. State-regulated psilocybin services explainer

**Before:** Psilocybin therapy is legal now, and one session can rewire your brain. Book before the program fills up.

**After:** Some jurisdictions authorize supervised psilocybin services under specific state rules; that is not the same as general legality, FDA approval, or a guaranteed clinical outcome. Check the current regulator source for eligibility and risks, and speak with a qualified clinician about individual health questions. You do not need to book anything to inspect the sources.

- Evidence: separates a jurisdiction-specific service program from general legality, FDA status, and clinical efficacy.
- Discussion: preserves jurisdiction, date, eligibility, risk, and outcome uncertainty.
- Self-Determination: removes neural and scarcity pressure and keeps source inspection independent of purchase.
- Action: routes legal status to the current regulator and personal health questions to qualified care.

## 2. Cannabis product education

**Before:** This strain melts anxiety without the dangerous side effects of prescriptions, and the algorithm loves honest wellness content.

**After:** Cannabis products vary in composition, labeling, legal status, and effects. Evidence does not support promising that a named product will treat an individual’s anxiety or be safer than prescribed medicine. Review the current certificate of analysis and jurisdiction rules; discuss symptoms, interactions, pregnancy, driving, or medication changes with a qualified clinician. Platform eligibility and distribution are separate and not guaranteed.

- Evidence: removes comparative-safety, treatment, and ranking assurances and names records that can actually be inspected.
- Discussion: preserves product variability, jurisdiction, interactions, and limits of comparison.
- Self-Determination: avoids fear of prescription treatment and does not pressure purchase.
- Action: assigns product/legal verification and individualized health decisions to the appropriate sources and professionals.

## 3. Prescription antidepressant education

**Before:** Antidepressants numb your brain. This natural alternative fixes the root cause, so taper off and take control.

**After:** Prescription antidepressants have different indications, effects, side effects, and discontinuation risks; an educational video cannot determine whether a medicine is appropriate for an individual. Do not start, stop, or taper medication from this content. Use the dated prescribing information as one source and make treatment changes only with the prescribing clinician; seek urgent help for immediate safety concerns.

- Evidence: replaces unsupported brain, naturalness, and root-cause claims with bounded medication information.
- Discussion: identifies indication, variability, adverse effects, discontinuation, and the limits of educational content.
- Self-Determination: removes shame and false autonomy framing while preserving informed questions.
- Action: routes medication changes and urgent safety concerns to accountable care.
