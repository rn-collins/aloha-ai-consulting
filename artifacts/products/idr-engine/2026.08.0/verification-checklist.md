# IDR verification checklist

- [ ] Question, jurisdiction, time, and use are frozen.
- [ ] Primary authority resolves and identity is recorded.
- [ ] Pinpoint supports the exact proposition.
- [ ] Mandatory/persuasive and primary/secondary labels are correct.
- [ ] Effective date, supersession, conflict, and procedural posture are checked.
- [ ] Retrieval date, confidence, limitations, and change watch are present.
- [ ] Adversarial fixtures do not pass as verified claims.
- [ ] Named human reviewer approves the bounded output.
