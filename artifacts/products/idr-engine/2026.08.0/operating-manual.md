# The IDR Engine

Version 2026.08.0 · evidence cutoff 2026-08-03 · frozen pre-release

Educational research-record system only. Not legal advice, not a continuously connected feed, and not a guarantee of currency, completeness, authority, or outcome. A qualified human must verify every consequential claim against the cited primary source and current law.

## Ingest → Digest → Regurgitate

**Ingest** defines the question, jurisdiction, time, and decision use; locates candidate authorities; ranks primary versus secondary and mandatory versus persuasive; and quarantines unresolved sources. **Digest** reads the primary text, records the exact proposition and pinpoint, resolves status and conflicts, assigns bounded confidence, and creates a change-watch trigger. **Regurgitate** emits only the proposition supported by the record, with authority weight, retrieval date, limitations, and human-review state visible. “Regurgitate” never means copying a model answer.

## Authority hierarchy and jurisdiction

1. Constitutions and controlling statutes. 2. Operative regulations and binding administrative materials. 3. Controlling judicial decisions. 4. Persuasive primary authorities. 5. Official secondary navigation. 6. Commentary and discovery aids. Lower layers can locate authority but cannot silently replace it. Binding force depends on forum, issuing body, date, procedural posture, and proposition.

## Production protocol

1. Freeze the research question and consequence. 2. Record every candidate in the authority ledger. 3. Resolve the primary text and pinpoint. 4. Run the fabricated, nonbinding, superseded, and jurisdiction tests. 5. Create the claim record. 6. Obtain named human verification. 7. Export only verified-for-bounded-use records. 8. Reverify on the trigger or before consequential use.

## Federal maintenance

Use FederalRegister.gov to discover and inspect documents, then preserve the official document identifier and published text. Reconcile amendments with current eCFR text and effective dates; eCFR is authoritative editorial material but not the legal edition of the CFR. Use GovInfo for authenticated or official published documents and preserve package/granule identifiers where available. Never infer current law from a search snippet.

## Worked failures

**Mata.** The process broke at Ingest because nonexistent authorities were allowed into the record, at Digest because quotations and existence were not verified, and at Regurgitate because fluent output was filed without adequate human verification. The court imposed a $5,000 penalty; consult the supplied order and current docket before relying on the characterization.

**Wadsworth.** The disclosed failure pattern is a filing containing eight nonexistent cases, followed by sanctions including revocation of the primary drafter's pro hac vice admission and monetary consequences. The supplied source is deliberately marked verify-document-version-before-use: the method requires the user to retrieve and pinpoint the operative order before repeating any amount or procedural detail.

## Human-review gate

No consequential output may leave the system unless humanReview.required is true, status is verified, the reviewer is identified, the authority resolves, the pinpoint supports the exact proposition, and the change watch is not overdue. The package has no live source connection and performs no automatic legal update.
