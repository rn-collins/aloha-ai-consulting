1. PRM-SOURCE-INTAKE
Purpose: source intake
Inputs: named record IDs; intended channel; human reviewer
Instruction: Convert a supplied source into a source record. Do not infer missing publisher, date, rights, or verification fields; mark them for human completion.
Output: Return structured findings with record IDs, unknowns, and required human decisions.
Boundary: Use only supplied records; no individualized professional advice or assurance.
