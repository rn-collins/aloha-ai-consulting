# Audit-Ready Operations

Version 2026.08.0 · evidence cutoff 2026-08-03 · frozen pre-release

Dated educational operations architecture only; not legal, compliance, clinical, privacy, or records-management advice. Qualified Oregon counsel or compliance personnel must verify the package against current Oregon law, OPS forms, TLC behavior, and the operator's facts before release or use.

## Three operating rules

1. Capture once, at the source, under a named owner. 2. Separate client-response data from service-center operational data. 3. Preserve the source, calculation, review, and submission evidence for every reported value.

## Current reporting cadence

OPS's guide updated February 24, 2026 describes four 25-day TLC windows: April 1–25 for Q1, July 1–25 for Q2, October 1–25 for Q3, and January 1–25 for Q4, with submission due by 11:59 p.m. on the 25th. The internal target in this package is five calendar days earlier and is not an official deadline. TLC does not autosave; save manually and preserve final submission confirmation.

## Opt-out boundary

Collect the current form from every administration-session client. If the client opts out, exclude their client-form responses from aggregated client data submitted to OPS. Do not erase them from service-center operational totals; report the total number of opt-outs. Never encourage or influence the choice.

## Workflow

Assign field owners; verify the current form, rules, guide, and TLC; capture records; lock the quarter; run validation and fixtures; obtain two-person review; enter and manually save TLC; confirm both client and service-center submissions; preserve confirmation and verification ledger; trigger correction review for any mismatch.
