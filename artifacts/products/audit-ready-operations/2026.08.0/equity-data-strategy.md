# Equity-data strategy

Use the current OHA REALD/SOGI-aligned form verbatim; provide accessible administration without influencing responses; separate “declined,” “unknown,” and missing according to the current form and TLC; minimize access; test small-cell and re-identification risks; honor opt-out for client-response submission; never use sensitive responses for marketing, eligibility, differential service, or inferred identity; review findings with affected communities and qualified privacy/compliance personnel.
