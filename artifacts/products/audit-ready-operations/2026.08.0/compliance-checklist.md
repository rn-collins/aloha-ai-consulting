# Quarterly compliance checklist

- [ ] Current 303 form, guide, rules, and TLC fields verified.
- [ ] Quarter scope and license status confirmed.
- [ ] Opt-outs separated from client-response aggregation and counted once in service-center data.
- [ ] Session, group, dose, denial, adverse, and post-session records reconciled.
- [ ] Zero denominators display NA rather than 0 or an error.
- [ ] Official deadline and internal target are separately labeled.
- [ ] Named preparer and independent reviewer approve.
- [ ] TLC manually saved, both submission states confirmed, evidence retained.
