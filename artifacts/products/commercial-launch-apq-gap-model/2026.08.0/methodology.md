## What APQ means here

APQ means **Authority, Posture-to-practice, and Quantification**. It is a state launch-readiness comparison rubric. It is separate from the DEA term “aggregate production quota.” No state score estimates federal supply, manufacturer quota, market size, probability of approval, or investment return.

7. Reverify before consequential use and issue a new immutable version when anything material changes.

## Federal boundary

State programs do not remove federal Controlled Substances Act risk. Public DEA aggregate production quota notices do not disclose confidential individual manufacturer quotas and must not be used as if they do.
