Source Desk: AI and prospective-client intake

This is the public audit trail for the material claims in “Should a small law firm use AI to summarize prospective-client intake?” It preserves the difference between controlling law, model authority, advisory guidance, vendor statements, empirical research, and RN’s bounded analysis.

Current disposition: human_led_only. No candidate product is nominated or tested. The 51-jurisdiction matrix is an acquisition-status baseline, not nationwide legal clearance.

How to read this desk

  • Claim ID maps to the canonical internal claim ledger.
  • Authority describes the source’s legal or evidentiary weight; it is not a quality score.
  • Support states what the cited source can establish.
  • Limit prevents the source from being stretched beyond its actual scope.
  • Verified is the last evidence-review date, not a promise that the law or product has not changed since.

Canonical inventory reconciled August 8, 2026: 83 claims, 108 sources, 78 verified pinpoint mappings, and 69 searches. This public view includes material release claims and omits duplicative acquisition records, sealed benchmark answers, and background sources that do not change the public decision.

Known limits and open gates

  • No candidate product, configuration, or firm workflow has been tested.
  • The frozen 24-record benchmark contains fictional cases and scoring rules, not observed results.
  • The 51-jurisdiction record tracks acquisition status and bounded propositions; it does not clear a real deployment.
  • All 15 cited public destinations were refreshed on August 8, 2026. Both PubMed destinations and record identities were verified, but their page surface presented a browser challenge; no new full-text proposition was added.
  • No independent legal review or legal clearance is claimed. The finite disposition is narrowed, attributed public claims; application to a real firm remains a separate controlling-law and facts inquiry.
  • Two first-party documentary visuals and their rights records are complete in draft. The real-environment photograph or omission disposition, assistive-technology QA, representative disabled-user review, any DOCX/tagged-PDF distribution exports, and RN release approval remain open.

Correction and reopening rule

If a source changes, a jurisdiction adopts new authority, or a vendor supplies a qualifying evidence packet, the affected claim must be reopened with its old wording, new evidence, decision owner, date, and downstream impact. A later product evaluation cannot retroactively convert this August 8, 2026 public-evidence finding into an endorsement or observed test.
