Source Desk: AI and prospective-client intake
This is the public audit trail for the material claims in “Should a small law firm use AI to summarize prospective-client intake?” It preserves the difference between controlling law, model authority, advisory guidance, vendor statements, empirical research, and RN’s bounded analysis.
Current disposition: human_led_only . No candidate product is nominated or tested. The 51-jurisdiction matrix is an acquisition-status baseline, not nationwide legal clearance.
How to read this desk
Claim ID maps to the canonical internal claim ledger.
Authority describes the source’s legal or evidentiary weight; it is not a quality score.
Support states what the cited source can establish.
Limit prevents the source from being stretched beyond its actual scope.
Verified is the last evidence-review date, not a promise that the law or product has not changed since.
Canonical inventory reconciled August 8, 2026: 83 claims, 108 sources, 78 verified pinpoint mappings, and 69 searches. This public view includes material release claims and omits duplicative acquisition records, sealed benchmark answers, and background sources that do not change the public decision.

C012 Adjacent summarization research identifies incompleteness and hallucination as distinct error modes. S019 · Nguyen et al. 
Primary empirical preprint Abstract and Appendix F error analysis. Patent corpus, not prospective-client intake; cannot supply an intake error rate or vendor finding. 2026-08-04 
C013 A legal-deposition prototype demonstrates source-linked verification but not operational effectiveness. S020 · Akter et al. 
Adjacent prototype research Sections 3.1–3.4 and conclusion. Nine public transcripts, LLM-based evaluators, no intake setting or user-outcome trial. 2026-08-04 
C014–C015 A complete evaluation must extend beyond one accuracy score and precede any live pilot. S021 · NIST AI 800-4 ; S022 · NIST AI RMF 1.0 
Official voluntary cross-sector frameworks NIST AI 800-4 pp. 1–2 and Table 1 pp. 6–7; lifecycle documentation and monitoring categories. Not intake-specific or mandatory. Firm thresholds are governance choices, not universal constants. 2026-08-04 
C016 A nominal human reviewer does not itself prove that errors will be caught. S023 · Kücking et al. ; S024 · Dratsch et al. 
Adjacent controlled experiments Study abstracts and reported experimental results. Clinical settings, not legal intake. Use to justify direct reviewer testing, not predict a law-firm error rate. 2026-08-04 
C017–C018 Accessibility must cover the whole intake pathway; automated checking alone is insufficient. S025 · U.S. DOJ web-accessibility guidance 
Official nonbinding technical assistance Forms, labels, instructions, errors, keyboard access, and automated-checker discussion. Precise obligations depend on entity, jurisdiction, and facts. Technical conformance is not user validation. 2026-08-04 
C019 Florida Opinion 24-1 addresses AI chatbots used for intake and disclosure that a prospect is communicating with AI. S006 · Florida Opinion 24-1 
Official advisory, nonbinding opinion Lawyer Advertising discussion, p. 7. Florida-specific; not nationwide law, summarization validation, or product approval. 2026-08-06
