What you will do

01 · Diagnose

Map the intake process before choosing a tool.

See which facts people must verify, which steps need human judgment, and where an AI summary could create risk.

02 · Evaluate

Test an AI summary using a fictional case.

Check what came from the source, what is missing, and what the AI should not assume.

03 · Decide

Leave with a clear decision and stop rules.

Your decision may be to test a limited use, keep the work human-led, or stop. This is not product approval.

How participation works

Your reasoning is part of the method.

Privately record an opening view, map actual work, surface exceptions, test what would make the answer wrong, and convert the final decision into an owner, stop rule, if–then action, and review trigger. Dissent may remain in the record.
