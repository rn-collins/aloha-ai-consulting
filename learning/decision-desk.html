The curriculum boundary.

The inaugural curriculum is a governed twelve-issue monthly sequence. Selecting it does not make any issue publishable: the first three remain R1 working drafts, Issues 04–12 remain R0 research questions, and every issue must independently reach R7 before an owner-authorized release.

The editorial boundary.

New Aloha AI newsletter issues are intended as paid editorial work. Existing Beehiiv material may remain public. Complete dossiers and tools are planned for subscribers; applying RN’s judgment to a subscriber’s own situation belongs in the clinic or private review.

No subscription can open until Beehiiv’s payment account, paid tier, paywalls, welcome sequence, archive, receipts, cancellation path, and test purchase are complete.
