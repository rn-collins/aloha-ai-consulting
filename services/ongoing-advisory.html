01Teams treating AI as an ongoing capabilityClose

Who it's for

You've moved past the one-off project. AI is now part of how you operate, and you need someone senior thinking about it continuously — not a full-time hire you can't justify, and not starting from scratch with a new vendor every quarter. You want continuity, judgment, and a hand on the wheel.

  • You have live AI work and more coming, and no internal senior owner.
  • You want the same partner who knows your context, not a rotating bench.
  • You'd rather have steady guidance than lurch from project to project.
02Momentum dies between projectsOpen

The problem it solves

A great Sprint or build lands, then attention drifts. Nobody owns what's next. Tools change, a new use case appears, the guardrails go stale, the team hits a wall and there's no one to ask. Hiring a full-time AI lead is expensive and hard to fill; hiring a fresh consultant each time means re-explaining your business every round.

A retainer keeps a senior partner engaged with your context, so priorities stay sharp, builds keep shipping, guardrails stay current, and your team always has someone to turn to. You get the leadership without the headcount.

03What a fractional lead actually doesOpen

What's included

Prioritization & roadmap upkeep

We keep your AI roadmap alive — re-ranking as the business and the tools change, so effort stays pointed at what matters.

Standing strategy time

Regular working sessions with you and your leads to make decisions and clear blockers.

Hands-on help

Direct support building, tuning, and troubleshooting workflows and assistants when your team gets stuck.

Guardrail maintenance

Keeping your safe-use policy and review process current as usage grows and rules shift.

Team enablement

Ongoing coaching so your people get better over time, not just once at a workshop.

On-call judgment

A senior voice to sanity-check a tool, a vendor, or a risky idea before you commit.

06Where a retainer earns its keepOpen

Best-fit clients

Great fit: a growing team with ongoing AI ambitions, no senior internal owner, and a preference for a partner who already knows the business. You value continuity and want steady forward motion.

Not the right fit yet: if you have a single, bounded need, a Strategy Sprint or Implementation project is more honest use of your money than a retainer. Advisory is for the long game, not a one-time job.
