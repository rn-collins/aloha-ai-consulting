01Owners and operators who are done guessingClose

Who it's for

You run a small or mid-size business, a professional practice, or a team inside a larger org. You keep hearing that you should "use AI," you've tried a few things, and none of it has stuck. You don't need another demo. You need someone to look at how you actually operate and tell you the truth about where this technology pays off and where it will waste your money.

  • You have 5–200 people and real workflows worth mapping.
  • You want a decision, not a science project.
  • You'd rather spend two weeks getting it right than two quarters drifting.
02Adoption stalls when nobody has ranked the workOpen

The problem it solves

Without a roadmap, AI spending scatters. One person builds a clever prompt, another buys a tool nobody adopts, a third pilot quietly dies. There's no shared view of which processes are high-volume, low-risk, and genuinely improvable — the only combination worth automating first.

The Sprint fixes the sequencing problem. We inventory your workflows, score each candidate on impact, effort, and risk, and hand you an ordered plan. You stop spreading effort thin and start compounding wins in the right order.

03A structured audit, run with youOpen

What's included

Discovery interviews

Two to four working sessions with you and the people who own the workflows, so the plan reflects how the business really runs — not an org chart.

Workflow inventory

A mapped list of repeatable processes across your team, with volume, time cost, and sensitivity noted for each.

Opportunity scoring

Every candidate scored on impact, effort, and risk using a transparent rubric you keep. No black boxes.

Risk and guardrail review

A first pass on where confidentiality, accuracy, or disclosure obligations mean AI needs limits — or shouldn't be used at all.

Tooling recommendation

A grounded view of which tools fit your budget, data posture, and team, with honest trade-offs.

Roadmap readout

A live walkthrough of the sequenced plan so your team leaves aligned, not just emailed a PDF.

04What you keep at the endOpen

Deliverables

  • A written AI roadmap: prioritized initiatives, sequenced across the next one to three quarters.
  • The scored opportunity matrix, so you can re-rank as things change.
  • A tooling shortlist with fit notes and rough cost ranges.
  • A one-page risk summary flagging where to slow down or bring in a professional.
  • A recorded readout and your slides, yours to keep and share internally.
You own everything. If you never work with us again, the roadmap still stands on its own. That's the point.

Timeline options

One-week focused audit

A single workflow area or department. Fastest path to a decision when the scope is already narrow.

Two-week standard Sprint

The default. Full workflow inventory, scoring, risk pass, and readout across the business.

Three-week extended Sprint

Adds deeper stakeholder interviews and a light proof-of-concept on the top-ranked workflow, so you see it work before you commit to a build.

06Where the Sprint pays off — and where it doesn'tOpen

Best-fit clients

Great fit: a services firm, agency, clinic, or operations team with recurring manual work and a genuine intent to change how it runs. You have a budget owner in the room and can commit a few hours of your team's time over two weeks.

Not the right fit yet: if you already know exactly what to build and just need it built, skip the Sprint and go straight to Workflow Design or Implementation. If you're just curious, the free University will take you a long way first.
