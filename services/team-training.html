01Teams where adoption is unevenClose

Who it's for

Some of your people are already using AI. Most aren't, or are using it timidly and inconsistently. You want a common baseline: everyone confident with the tools you actually use, working from the same rules, producing work you'd be comfortable putting your name on.

  • You have a team of roughly 5 to 50 who'd benefit from shared skills.
  • You want practice on your work, not slides about someone else's.
  • You care as much about judgment — when not to use AI — as about mechanics.
02Generic training doesn't transferOpen

The problem it solves

The webinar everyone half-watches teaches AI in the abstract, so nobody knows how it applies to their inbox, their reports, their client work. People nod along, close the tab, and change nothing. Worse, untrained enthusiasm creates real risk: confidential data pasted into the wrong tool, hallucinated facts shipped to clients, no one checking anything.

These workshops fix both problems. We train on your workflows with your examples, so the skills stick — and we teach the guardrails alongside the techniques, so confidence doesn't outrun judgment.

03Built around your work, delivered liveOpen

What's included

Pre-session scoping

A short intake to learn your team's roles, tools, and the tasks worth practicing, so the session isn't generic.

Live, hands-on delivery

Remote or in person. People work through real tasks in the room, not watch a lecture.

Role-relevant exercises

Prompts and workflows drawn from your actual work — sales follow-ups, drafting, research, whatever your team does.

Safe-use fundamentals

Confidentiality, verification, and disclosure taught as part of the craft, not an afterthought.

Take-home prompt pack

A tailored set of prompts and checklists your team keeps and reuses.

Follow-up Q&A

A short session a couple of weeks later to answer the questions that only surface once people start using it.

04What the team keepsOpen

Deliverables

  • A live workshop tailored to your team's roles and tools.
  • A take-home prompt pack and quick-reference checklists.
  • A short safe-use guide covering confidentiality and verification.
  • The session recording, where the format allows, for anyone who missed it or wants a refresher.
  • A follow-up Q&A session to lock in the habits.
Training teaches good practice; it isn't legal, medical, or financial advice, and it doesn't replace professional review where your work requires it.
05Pick the format that fitsOpen

Timeline options

Single workshop

A focused half-day or shorter session on one theme — fundamentals, or one department's workflows.

Multi-session series

Two to four sessions spaced out, building from fundamentals to role-specific practice, so skills compound.

Train-the-champions

A deeper cohort for a handful of internal leads who then carry the practice across the rest of the organization.

06Where training lands hardestOpen

Best-fit clients

Great fit: a team that has the tools (or will) and needs the shared skill and shared rules to use them well. Leadership wants adoption to be consistent and safe, not a free-for-all.

Not the right fit yet: if your team has no tools and no plan, a Strategy Sprint should come first. And if you're an individual rather than a team, the free University lessons cover the fundamentals in full.
