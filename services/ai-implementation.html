Who it's for

You have a design you trust — from our Workflow Design, a Strategy Sprint, or your own work — and you want it built by someone who will do it carefully and won't leave you stranded when it breaks. You don't have a spare engineer to burn on this, or you'd rather they stayed on the core product.

  • You have a clear scope, or a design we can build from.
  • You want it tested on your actual data and workflows, not a toy demo.
  • You care about being able to run and maintain it without us.
02The gap between "we should" and "it works"Open

The problem it solves

Plenty of teams have a good AI idea and no clean way to ship it. The internal build stalls between other priorities. The freelancer delivers something that works once and nobody can maintain. The tool gets configured badly and quietly produces wrong answers that no one catches.

Implementation closes that gap with a disciplined build: configured to your design, tested against real inputs and edge cases, wrapped in the review checkpoints that keep it safe, and documented so your team can operate and change it. We build it to be handed over — not to keep you dependent.

03A build you can actually stand behindOpen

What's included

Configuration & build

The assistant, workflow, or system assembled to spec on tools that fit your budget and data posture.

Grounding in your material

Where relevant, we connect the system to your documents and examples so it answers from your reality, not the open internet.

Testing on real work

We run it against genuine inputs — including the awkward ones — and tune until it holds up.

Guardrails & review points

The human checkpoints and stop conditions from the design, wired in so the system fails safely.

Documentation

Plain-language runbooks: how it works, how to change it, and what to watch for.

Handover & enablement

A working session so your team can operate, adjust, and troubleshoot it confidently.

04What you keep at the endOpen

Deliverables

  • The working system, live in your environment and under your accounts.
  • Configuration files, prompts, and settings — documented and yours.
  • A test log showing how it performed on real inputs, including edge cases.
  • Operator and maintenance runbooks in plain language.
  • A recorded handover session and a short post-launch support window.
Built on your accounts, owned by you. No lock-in, no hostage data. If we part ways, the system keeps working.

06Where a build is the right moveOpen

Best-fit clients

Great fit: you have a validated design or a scope we can lock quickly, real work for the system to prove itself on, and a team that will actually use it. You value maintainability over a flashy prototype.

Not the right fit yet: if the design is still fuzzy, do Workflow Design first — building on a shaky design is how projects overrun. If you're not sure it's worth building at all, the Strategy Sprint answers that cheaply.
