In depth

Seven weekend-length courses where you build a working, governed AI system you control — a citation verifier, a supervised operator, a claims checker, and more. No coding background needed. Every course ships with a built-in AI tutor grounded only in the lessons.

Three ways in

Learn it, build it with us, or have it built

Each course is the entry point to the same system at three levels of support. Start where you are and move up the ladder when you want speed or a done-for-you result.

The Course

Async and self-paced with lifetime access. Nine or ten build-modules, templates, a rubric, and the governed AI tutor. You build your own working system.

Cohort / Lab

Build the same system alongside Aloha AI with live guidance and review. For teams and people who want the result faster with support.

Built & Governed

Aloha AI builds, integrates, and governs the system for you, with monitoring, evaluation, and an audit trail. Optional ongoing maintenance.

The catalog

Systems worth building

New to this? Start with the weekend on-ramp below. The Governed Operator is the flagship, and the one the other Aloha AI systems and course tutors run on.

Build Your First AI Team (in a Weekend)

The cheap, fast way in. Build a small, supervised team of named agents around one real workflow — with a role contract per agent and an approval step so nothing sends or spends without you. No coding background needed.

Build a Governed AI Operator

A supervised AI that researches, drafts, and organizes across your tools in your voice, and never sends, posts, or spends without your approval. The deepest build, and the architecture the rest run on.

Trust-Safe Citation Verifier

Catch fabricated, mis-stated, and unsupported citations before they ship. Checks the claim, not just the link.

Regulated Claims Checker

Screen marketing, product, and label claims against the rules that apply before you publish.

AI Workflow Audit

Map a business, score where AI fits, classify the risk, and output a defensible roadmap you can sell.

Legislative & Regulatory Tracker

Monitor bills and rules across jurisdictions, score what matters, and summarize each change with a citation.

Knowledge Base Readiness

Assess and prepare your knowledge base so an AI assistant or RAG system uses it safely and accurately.

AI Teams for Business

Have Aloha AI build and govern the system as a service, on the main practice site.
