The responsibility boundary

Evidence, method, assumptions, and limits stay visible.

Education and support do not silently become professional advice or delegated judgment.

Evidence

  • Canonical University resource registry
  • Resource audience and maturity metadata
  • Assessment recommendation mappings

Method

  1. Route by learner context
  2. Prefer the smallest useful first step
  3. Keep implementation and governance connected

Assumptions

  • The learner can identify a current role, goal, or bottleneck

Limitations

  • Routing is directional and cannot account for every organizational constraint
