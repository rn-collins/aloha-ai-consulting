01The teaching philosophy+

Most AI training sells a feeling and skips the mechanics. We do the opposite. We teach how the models work, where they break, and how to verify what they produce, drawn from primary sources rather than secondhand hype. When you understand why a tool behaves the way it does, you stop fearing it and you stop over-trusting it. You use it well.

02The neuroscience of trust+

Teams do not adopt tools they do not trust, and trust is not a slogan. It is what your brain does when a new tool proves reliable, repeatedly, in ways you can check. So this program is built around verification and honest limits. Every claim you can test, we show you how to test. Every place the tool fails, we name it. That is how adoption actually sticks: by making reliability visible.
