Before you begin

Rule mapping is a reasoned join among the claim, product classification, intended use, audience, medium, jurisdiction, and authority scope. Keyword overlap alone cannot establish that a rule governs.

Return every plausible rule, the mapping rationale, conflicts, and missing scope facts. Require human resolution when multiple regimes or product classifications remain possible.

This lesson is educational. Test important claims against current primary sources and use qualified review when the stakes require it.
