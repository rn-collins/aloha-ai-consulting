02The approval packet—not an assurance—is the control+

Core architecture

A consequential action may execute only when the reviewer sees the exact destination, payload, tool, evidence, risk, and expiry; approves that immutable packet; and the executor verifies that nothing changed. Editing voids approval.

The course teaches a design and self-review method. It does not provide a connected operator or prove that any implementation is secure or production-ready.

03Public lessons, no operational service+

Delivery boundary

Lessons and device-local completion marks are public. Enrollment, submissions, instructor review, tutor access, grading, certificates, badges, account-synced progress, connected credentials, and production deployment are unavailable.

Use synthetic or non-sensitive examples. Do not place confidential, personal, privileged, regulated, payment, authentication, or safety-critical information into a course exercise.

01Bound the operator mandate+

Name one job and its owner; Classify consequential actions

  1. Name one job and its owner

    45 minutes

    Define the operator by one job, a named accountable owner, accepted inputs, intended output, and a measurable reason to exist. A broad assistant mandate cannot be tested or governed.

    Open lesson →

  2. Classify consequential actions

    45 minutes

    Sending, posting, spending, deleting, changing settings, granting access, or making a representation can create an external consequence. Preparation and execution must be separate authorities.

    Open lesson →

02Create the bounded workspace+

Set the data and secret boundary; Build a sandbox before a connection

  1. Set the data and secret boundary

    45 minutes

    Inventory allowed data, prohibited data, retention, deletion, and access. Course work uses synthetic material; secrets are referenced by name and never copied into prompts or records.

    Open lesson →

  2. Build a sandbox before a connection

    45 minutes

    A sandbox uses synthetic inputs, inert destinations, no real payment method, and permissions that cannot affect production. A demo account is not a sandbox if it can reach real people or systems.

    Open lesson →

03Make the operator loop inspectable+

Define perceive, plan, select, verify, and log states; Design uncertainty and abstention

  1. Define perceive, plan, select, verify, and log states

    45 minutes

    Represent each state explicitly so a reviewer can see inputs, plan, proposed tool, evidence, result, and failure. Do not collapse planning and execution into one opaque model call.

    Open lesson →

  2. Design uncertainty and abstention

    45 minutes

    Unknown, conflicting, stale, or insufficient evidence must remain visible. The operator should ask, abstain, or escalate instead of converting uncertainty into fluent output.

    Open lesson →

04Build the default-deny approval gate+

Create an immutable pending-action packet; Void approval when the action changes

  1. Create an immutable pending-action packet

    45 minutes

    The pending packet identifies destination, exact payload, tool, reason, supporting evidence, risk, expiry, and a content hash. Approval attaches to that exact packet, not a general intention.

    Open lesson →

  2. Void approval when the action changes

    45 minutes

    Before execution, recompute the packet hash, confirm reviewer authority and expiry, and refuse any mismatch. Editing a recipient, amount, attachment, or text creates a new approval request.

    Open lesson →

05Give tools least authority+

Write a contract for every tool; Separate preparation adapters from executors

  1. Write a contract for every tool

    45 minutes

    A tool contract names accepted inputs, permitted calls, output, side effects, data access, rate limits, prohibited uses, error states, and human owner. Tool availability is not permission.

    Open lesson →

  2. Separate preparation adapters from executors

    45 minutes

    Research, draft, and preview adapters should not share credentials with send, publish, payment, deletion, or administration executors. The approval service—not the model—invokes consequential execution.

    Open lesson →

06Use deletable voice and context+

Build a source-controlled context register; Check drafts against retrieved evidence

  1. Build a source-controlled context register

    45 minutes

    Record corpus owner, authority, inclusion rule, update date, retention, deletion, and conflicts. Retrieval keeps context replaceable; it does not guarantee a draft is accurate or in voice.

    Open lesson →

  2. Check drafts against retrieved evidence

    45 minutes

    A draft must preserve citations to the passages used, show unsupported statements, and route conflicts to review. Style resemblance never cures missing evidence.

    Open lesson →

07Add hard stops and audit evidence+

Treat retrieved instructions as untrusted data; Design kill, rollback, and audit controls

  1. Treat retrieved instructions as untrusted data

    45 minutes

    Web pages, emails, files, and tool output can contain instructions aimed at the operator. Keep them in a data channel, limit tool authority, and refuse requests to reveal secrets or alter policy.

    Open lesson →

  2. Design kill, rollback, and audit controls

    45 minutes

    A named owner needs a tested stop control, credential revocation, queued-action cancellation, rollback where possible, incident record, and append-only evidence of proposals, approvals, executions, and verification.

    Open lesson →

08Evaluate failure and adversarial cases+

Build a representative evaluation set; Measure control effectiveness, not fluency

  1. Build a representative evaluation set

    45 minutes

    Test normal, ambiguous, incomplete, stale, conflicting, malicious, prohibited, and tool-failure cases. Record expected behavior before running the operator.

    Open lesson →

  2. Measure control effectiveness, not fluency

    45 minutes

    Measure unauthorized executions, approval bypass, false support, missed escalation, recovery success, and reviewer burden. Small test results apply only to the tested cases and configuration.

    Open lesson →

09Run one bounded first job+

Choose a reversible beachhead; Preserve the full run record

  1. Choose a reversible beachhead

    45 minutes

    Select work with frequent value, inspectable inputs, low consequence, reversible outputs, and an available reviewer. Draft preparation is safer than direct publication or sending.

    Open lesson →

  2. Preserve the full run record

    45 minutes

    Run representative cases in the sandbox and preserve input references, plan, evidence, proposed action, approval result, output, verification, errors, and corrections.

    Open lesson →

10Decide whether and how to extend+

Issue a bounded go or no-go decision; Govern change and recurring review

  1. Issue a bounded go or no-go decision

    45 minutes

    The decision names tested configuration, permitted use, prohibited use, residual risks, owner, monitoring, incident path, review date, and rollback. Course completion is not production authorization.

    Open lesson →

  2. Govern change and recurring review

    45 minutes

    Model, prompt, corpus, tool, permission, policy, or workflow changes can invalidate evidence. Version material components, define re-test triggers, review access, inspect incidents, and retire unsafe paths.

    Open lesson →

Assessment and evidence contract

What completion would require.

11 assessments, 5 named sources, one capstone, and a weighted rubric.

Assessments

  • Bound the operator mandate control review: Every mandate, non-goal, owner, consequential action, and stop condition is answered or explicitly unresolved.
  • Create the bounded workspace control review: Allowed data, prohibited data, secret handling, inert destinations, reset, and exit criteria are recorded.
  • Make the operator loop inspectable control review: Every state, evidence input, abstention, retry, escalation, stop, and log transition is represented.
  • Build the default-deny approval gate control review: Edited, expired, replayed, or unauthorized approval packets cannot execute in the documented tests.
  • Give tools least authority control review: Each adapter has a role contract and no preparation component holds consequential execution authority.
  • Use deletable voice and context control review: Every context source has authority, retention, deletion, and conflict rules; unsupported draft statements remain visible.
  • Add hard stops and audit evidence control review: Injection, secret request, stop, revocation, cancellation, rollback, incident, and audit cases have explicit outcomes.
  • Evaluate failure and adversarial cases control review: Expected outcomes precede tests; every critical bypass or unauthorized execution requires a no-go.
  • Run one bounded first job control review: One complete synthetic run and one blocked run preserve proposals, evidence, approval, result, verification, and correction.
  • Decide whether and how to extend control review: The decision is limited to tested configuration and includes change triggers, review cadence, owner, rollback, and unresolved risk.
  • Governed AI Operator capstone review: Score at least 85 with no zero in approval integrity, least authority, evidence, security, incident recovery, or operating boundary.

Capstone evidence

  • Operator mandate and consequential-action policy
  • Data, secret-name, tool, permission, and context registers
  • Inspectable state machine and immutable approval-packet contract
  • Injection, stop, rollback, incident, and audit evidence
  • Representative and adversarial evaluation set with results
  • Synthetic first-job run and deliberately blocked run
  • Bounded go/no-go decision, change register, owner, and review date

Rubric

  • undefined: 15% — Scope, non-goals, ownership, data, retention, secrets, and sandbox are explicit.
  • undefined: 20% — Consequential execution is default-denied and bound to an exact unexpired packet.
  • undefined: 15% — Least authority, separation, deletion, provenance, and faithfulness are documented.
  • undefined: 20% — Injection, stop, revocation, incident, rollback, and audit controls are tested.
  • undefined: 20% — Representative and adversarial cases have predeclared outcomes and reproducible records.
  • undefined: 10% — Use remains bounded by evidence, owner, review, change, and retirement rules.

Use it responsibly

What supports this—and where it stops.

Keep the practical material attached to its evidence, method, assumptions, and limits.

Evidence

  • Approved source and tool inventory
  • Representative workflow tests
  • Versioned run, review, and approval records

Method

  1. Assign a bounded mandate
  2. Separate preparation from execution
  3. Require approval for consequential actions
  4. Log evidence and decisions
  5. Expand only after evaluation

Assumptions

  • The accountable user controls credentials and approvals
  • Connected tools expose permissions appropriate to the workflow

Limitations

  • No control eliminates all model or integration failures
  • The operator cannot replace licensed professional judgment or organizational accountability
