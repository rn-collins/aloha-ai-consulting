Before you begin

A citation is usually attached to a sentence, clause, quotation, or string of propositions. Capture enough context for review while preserving the exact proposition being tested.

When one sentence contains multiple claims or citations, split the review units or mark the mapping ambiguous. Do not let proximity silently become support.

This lesson is educational. Test important claims against current primary sources and use qualified review when the stakes require it.
