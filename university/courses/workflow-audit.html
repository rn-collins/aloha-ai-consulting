Capstone evidence

  • Dated scope, exclusions, reliance boundary, participants, owners, and source register
  • Canonical workflow maps with representative artifacts, exceptions, failures, and dissent
  • Separate value, feasibility, consequence, confidence, and critical-control rationales
  • Pilot contract, baseline, evaluation set, thresholds, review, incident, and rollback plan
  • Sequenced roadmap with no-go findings, dependencies, owners, costs, review dates, and release approval

Rubric

  • undefined: 20% — Workflow records reflect actual work, variation, exceptions, systems, and owners.
  • undefined: 20% — Value, feasibility, consequence, confidence, and blocks are evidenced and separable.
  • undefined: 20% — Data, access, authority, rights, security, legal, and recordkeeping questions are resolved or blocking.
  • undefined: 20% — Baselines, representative and adverse cases, thresholds, incidents, and rollback are testable.
  • undefined: 20% — No-go, repair, pilot, and scale decisions are sequenced with owners and review dates.

Use it responsibly

What supports this—and where it stops.

Keep the practical material attached to its evidence, method, assumptions, and limits.

Evidence

  • Dated audit scope, workflow register, and source register
  • Representative inputs, outputs, policies, systems, exceptions, and failure cases
  • Score rationales, uncertainty, dissent, evaluation evidence, approvals, and roadmap decisions

Method

  1. Map work before selecting tools
  2. Separate value, feasibility, consequence, and confidence
  3. Keep critical controls outside composite scores
  4. Prioritize reversible pilots with explicit evaluation gates
  5. Turn findings into owned decisions and review dates

Assumptions

  • Authorized workflow owners and affected participants provide representative evidence
  • Qualified domain, privacy, security, legal, and technical reviewers are available where the use requires them

Limitations

  • The method cannot establish universal or certified AI-readiness and does not replace industry-specific professional review
  • Findings are only as reliable as the bounded evidence, participation, authority records, and evaluation available on the review date
