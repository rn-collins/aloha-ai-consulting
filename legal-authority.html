The governing terms

Open the section you need.

6 documented sections. Named Aloha AI resources link directly to their canonical pages.

01Professional boundary+

RN Collins is a JD candidate, not a licensed attorney. Aloha AI provides research, education, workflow design, and technical or operational support; it does not provide legal advice, create an attorney-client relationship, make a legal determination, or replace counsel responsible for a matter. A licensed attorney must control legal judgment, advice, representation, filing, and sign-off whenever the task or jurisdiction requires one.

02Source hierarchy+

For a legal-status statement, use the constitution, enacted law, filed rule or regulation, official court opinion, docketed order, or official agency action with jurisdiction before secondary summaries. Official codifications and government repositories are preferred access points. Secondary sources may help locate or explain authority but do not silently replace controlling primary authority.

03Jurisdiction, version, and effective state+

A registered statement identifies the governing jurisdiction, authority type, official source, retrieval date, and effective-state status. Proposed, introduced, pending, stayed, vacated, superseded, repealed, expired, and effective authorities are not interchangeable. A date on a page establishes when it was checked, not that it remains current today.

04Conflicts and negative treatment+

When authorities conflict, the record preserves the conflict and routes the issue for qualified review; it does not average the sources or select the preferred outcome silently. Later, higher, controlling, jurisdiction-specific, or more directly applicable authority may change the result. Case treatment, amendments, effective dates, exceptions, definitions, and incorporated materials must be checked before reliance.

05What tools and explainers may do+

A browser-local flag, lexical match, source link, schedule record, citation-format result, summary, or review question is triage evidence only. It does not establish existence, good-law status, legal effect, applicability, permission, prohibition, compliance, eligibility, liability, or a complete answer. No match is not clearance.

06Review and correction+

The public authority register at /api/legal-authority-register.json records only bounded claims selected for authority review; it is not a complete inventory of every legal phrase on the site. A material change, conflict, stale review, broken official source, or correction report reopens the record. Use the Corrections route for a possible error and qualified counsel for reliance in a real matter.

Accountability record

Evidence, method, assumptions, and limits.

The public record includes the conditions that limit how it should be read.

Evidence

  • A machine-readable source register with jurisdiction, authority, retrieval, effective-state, and review fields
  • A public professional-accountability and non-advice boundary
  • A release-blocking evaluator for source hierarchy, conflicts, staleness, and prohibited inferences

Method

  1. Frame the precise proposition and jurisdiction
  2. Retrieve and preserve controlling primary authority
  3. Record version, effective state, conflicts, and limitations
  4. Route legal judgment and reliance to the responsible licensed professional

Assumptions

  • Official source links and recorded retrieval facts accurately identify the material inspected
  • The deployed site matches the evaluated repository tree

Limitations

  • The register is selected and bounded rather than a complete legal-research audit of the site
  • Authority may change after retrieval
  • The process does not establish legal advice, attorney-client relationship, good-law status, applicability, or compliance
