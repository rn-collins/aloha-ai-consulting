Do not skip this part

Output and boundary belong together.

A useful result is still conditional on its evidence, method, assumptions, and limitations.

Evidence

  • The intended business role and representative tasks
  • Applicable data, tool, approval, retention, and escalation requirements
  • Named human ownership and evaluation records

Method

  1. Define one bounded role
  2. Specify permitted inputs, tools, and outputs
  3. Prohibit consequential autonomous actions
  4. Attach evidence, confidence, approval, and escalation rules
  5. Assign logging, retention, evaluation, failure response, and a human owner

Assumptions

  • The organization will test the contract against representative work
  • Permissions and technical controls will enforce the written boundaries

Limitations

  • A written role contract does not itself enforce permissions or establish compliance
  • Qualified legal, privacy, security, and domain review may still be required
