## Portfolio progression

- Quarter 1: information enters or leaves a boundary—intake, meetings, customer communication.
- Quarter 2: organizational knowledge and consequential representation—retrieval, opportunity allocation, public claims.
- Quarter 3: meaning, identity, and delegated power—high-stakes explanation, synthetic identity, agent action.
- Quarter 4: institutional control over the lifecycle—vendor choice, learner-affecting use, continuation and retirement.

This sequence moves from a bounded workflow through increasingly consequential authority and ends with lifecycle governance. It is designed to avoid twelve versions of the same “AI risk” class.
