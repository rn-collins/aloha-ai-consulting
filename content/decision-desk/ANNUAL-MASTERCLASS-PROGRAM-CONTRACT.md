# Decision Desk annual masterclass program contract

Status: governing internal production plan; not a public cadence, sale, or release authorization  
Owner: RN Collins / Aloha AI  
Program window: September 2026–August 2027  
Adopted from owner direction: 2026-08-08  
Review cadence: after every delivered session and at least quarterly

## Decision

Aloha AI will prepare a twelve-month Decision Desk masterclass program: one bounded masterclass per month, each built from one governed Decision Desk question and one reusable decision tool.

This contract changes Issues 02–12 from an indefinite expansion roadmap to planned production units. It does not claim that those issues are researched, rehearsed, accessible, scheduled publicly, or ready to sell. Issue 01 remains the only near-release candidate at adoption.

## Program promise

The internal program goal is twelve distinct 90-minute working masterclasses. Participants practice how to decide whether, where, and under what controls an AI use belongs in real work. The series is not a product-tour sequence, generalized AI literacy course, legal advice service, CLE program, certification, or guarantee of outcomes.

A monthly slot is a production target, not permission to publish. No date, price, seat, checkout, or communication becomes public until that month's release gate passes and RN approves it.

## Standard monthly release unit

Every month must contain:

1. a bounded governed question and audience;
2. a current, claim-level evidence package with contradictions and limits;
3. a Decision Desk article and public Source Desk;
4. one reusable decision instrument;
5. an exact product/configuration evaluation when lawful and evidentially possible, or a transparent no-product/no-test finding;
6. a complete 90-minute teaching package;
7. accessible participant, preparation, follow-up, and correction materials;
8. a bounded clinic translation or an explicit reason the clinic is inappropriate;
9. an inactive visitor journey and complete commercial/communications specification;
10. visual-evidence and rights records proportionate to the claims;
11. observed technical, editorial, accessibility, operational, and rehearsal QA within recorded scopes; and
12. RN go/no-go approval.

Issue-specific completion contracts may narrow irrelevant elements but may not waive factual integrity, privacy, security, accessibility, rights, operational testing, or RN approval.

## Standard teaching architecture

Each class must produce participant work, not passive exposure:

| Segment | Time | Required participant output |
|---|---:|---|
| Decision and stakes | 0–10 | bounded decision statement |
| Authority, people, and data | 10–25 | authority/affected-person map |
| Workflow before product | 25–40 | redrawn current-state workflow |
| Product and configuration evidence | 40–55 | evidence and diligence gaps |
| Fictional or safely synthetic exercise | 55–72 | completed issue-specific instrument |
| Safeguards, escalation, and stop rules | 72–82 | control set |
| Disposition | 82–90 | bounded next-action record |

No exercise may require participants to disclose client, employer, health, financial, student, customer, or other confidential/personal facts.

## Rolling production system

For session month M:

- M-12 weeks: activate issue; approve audience, decision, completion contract, displacement, and research owner.
- M-10 weeks: freeze research protocol, source classes, affected-person evidence plan, and product-access decision.
- M-8 weeks: evidence checkpoint; resolve unsupported claims, legal-review need, and visual permissions.
- M-6 weeks: freeze article thesis, instrument specification, fictional case design, and no-product fallback.
- M-4 weeks: teaching, participant, clinic, visitor, and communications packages reach complete draft.
- M-3 weeks: factual/editorial/legal-boundary reconciliation; accessible delivery formats generated.
- M-2 weeks: commercial test mode, browser/accessibility inspection, and rehearsal 1.
- M-1 week: defect correction, rehearsal 2, support/incident readiness, and RN go/no-go.
- M+2 business days: participant follow-up and corrections path.
- M+10 business days: retrospective, evidence/incidents log, and next-month scope correction.

Months overlap. At least three issue states must be visible at all times: current delivery, next-month build, and following-month research.

## Capacity and quality controls

- RN is the substantive owner and default facilitator.
- A named day-of support/technical operator is required before any live session.
- Default first-cohort capacity is 12 unless a month-specific exercise requires fewer.
- Standard price may inherit the Issue 01 recommendation, but every month requires an explicit price and value review; no annual subscription is implied.
- Two complete rehearsals are required for a new issue. A repeated, unchanged session may use one delta rehearsal only after an observed successful delivery.
- Critical or high defects block release.
- Evidence freshness, product configuration, policy links, and participant-facing claims are rechecked within seven days of delivery.

## Honest substitution and deferral rule

If a monthly unit cannot pass its evidence, rights, accessibility, operational, rehearsal, or approval gate:

1. defer that unit;
2. use a previously completed masterclass only if it is still current, distinctly labeled, and RN approves the repeat;
3. use a fully ready reserve unit if one exists; or
4. leave the month dark.

A calendar target never authorizes thin research, invented testing, unlicensed visuals, inaccessible files, untested transactions, or misleading readiness language.

## Annual program QA

The annual system must maintain:

- a single issue/status register;
- versioned shared templates with controlled month-specific overrides;
- claim, source, pinpoint, contradiction, search, visual, rights, product, and incident records;
- a master production calendar and dependency register;
- cross-month curriculum coverage and duplication review;
- participant feedback and correction trends without collecting sensitive case facts;
- financial reconciliation by session;
- accessibility findings and unresolved barriers;
- change monitoring for time-sensitive authorities, products, vendors, and policies; and
- a quarterly continue/redesign/pause decision.

## Explicitly unclaimed at adoption

This contract does not establish that:

- Issues 02–12 have completed research;
- any future date or price is public or final;
- any provider, transaction, email, calendar, or support workflow is configured;
- any class has been rehearsed or delivered;
- accessibility has been validated by representative users;
- photography or third-party rights have been obtained;
- independent legal review occurred; or
- RN approved release of any month.
