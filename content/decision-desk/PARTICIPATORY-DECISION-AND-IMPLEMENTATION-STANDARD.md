# Participatory decision and implementation standard

Status: canonical method standard; active; not release authorization  
Owner: RN Collins / Aloha AI  
Adopted: 2026-08-09

## Governing promise

RN supplies the bounded question, evidence discipline, facilitation structure, challenge function, and synthesis. Participants externalize the contextual knowledge that lives in practice, test the map against reality, make or route decisions within their authority, and own the actions required to use and maintain the result. RN does not privately manufacture an answer for passive receipt.

Participation is consequential. The record must show what participant knowledge changed, what remains disputed, who owns each decision, and how the result will be appraised after use.

## Required method

1. Bound one decision, its owner, affected people, authority, exclusions, and expiration trigger.
2. Collect each participant’s view privately before discussion.
3. Map actual work: sources, handoffs, workarounds, exceptions, incentives, judgment, access needs, and downstream reliance.
4. Reconcile perspectives; mark agreement, disagreement, missing roles, and assertions needing evidence.
5. Separate authority, policy, vendor statement, observation, participant assertion, RN analysis, and unknown.
6. Invite disconfirming evidence, failure conditions, affected-person perspectives, and the strongest reasonable alternative.
7. Have participants or their named organizational owner make the decision within their authority.
8. Convert it into owners, dates, observable evidence, stop conditions, escalation, and if–then actions.
9. Set a review date or change trigger and a route for defects, exceptions, affected-person feedback, and corrections.
10. Version corrections without silently rewriting the prior record.

## Psychological-safety and anti-groupthink controls

- individual input precedes open discussion;
- uncertainty, dissent, and unresolved are valid states;
- senior or fast voices do not answer first where hierarchy may distort disclosure;
- written, private, asynchronous, and alternative-format contribution remain available;
- airtime is not treated as influence;
- the facilitator asks what would make the current answer wrong;
- minority views and unresolved disagreement remain in the record;
- confidential, privileged, identifying, regulated, or security-sensitive facts are prohibited;
- pause, recusal, withdrawal, and fictionalization remain available without penalty.

Psychological safety does not mean comfort, agreement, guaranteed group confidentiality, or absence of accountability.

## Required applied-offer record

- actual-work and authoritative-source maps;
- assumptions, exceptions, dependencies, incentives, affected people, and dissent;
- decision owner and participant decision rights;
- bounded disposition and what it does not authorize;
- owners, dates, and observable completion evidence;
- if–then plan: “If [trigger], [owner] will [action], record it in [location], and escalate to [role] within [time]”;
- stop conditions and stop authority;
- review date/change trigger, maintenance owner, and correction route.

## Measurement

Measure completion and evidence distinctions; opening-to-final decision change; surfaced exceptions, dissent, and unknowns; owner acceptance; action completion; observed use, defects, stops, corrections, and review completion; access barriers; and participation balance. Report comprehension/confidence separately from correctness. Attendance, satisfaction, or a completed worksheet alone is not implementation.
