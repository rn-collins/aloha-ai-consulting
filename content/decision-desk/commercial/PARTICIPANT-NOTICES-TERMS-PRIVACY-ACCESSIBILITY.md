# Participant notices, terms, privacy, and accessibility surface specification

Status: draft content architecture; not legal approval  
Version: 0.1

## Pre-purchase disclosure block

Every offer surface must present, before the purchase control: seller identity; exact offer; price/currency/taxes and fees; date/time/timezone; format; capacity where material; inclusions/exclusions; preparation; cancellation/refund/transfer/no-show/organizer-change rules; support; accessibility request route; privacy summary; recording rule; educational and no-legal-advice boundary; and links to full terms and privacy notice.

No accreditation, CLE, certification, affiliation, guaranteed outcome, product endorsement, or recurring-subscription claim may appear.

## Terms surface

Terms must cover eligibility, purchase formation, screen/acceptance distinction, participant conduct, confidentiality limits in group settings, prohibited submissions, intellectual-property permissions for supplied materials, platform requirements, cancellation/refund/transfer, organizer changes, access alternatives, recording prohibition, deliverable/correction boundary, complaint route, and governing legal-review disposition.

## Privacy surface

The just-in-time notice must identify data categories, purposes, recipients/processors, required versus optional fields, retention periods, access/correction/deletion routes, security and incident contact, cross-service data flow, and optional marketing separation. Exact retention periods and processors must be filled before collection. Rejected clinic descriptions are deleted promptly after the published appeal/refund period unless a documented obligation requires retention.

## Accessibility surface

A private request route must appear before purchase and in every operational message. Participants need not disclose diagnosis. Publish response time, available formats, caption/keyboard/untimed participation options, known platform constraints, and how unresolved needs affect cancellation/refund deadlines. Access requests are not marketing data.

## Version and evidence controls

Record the exact terms, notice, offer, form, consent, and refund-policy versions accepted by each participant. A material change cannot be silently applied retroactively. Legal review, accessibility QA, and privacy/security review must each record scope, reviewer, date, findings, and disposition.

## Placeholder gate

No public/commercial surface may launch with unresolved braces, sample contacts, assumed retention periods, unapproved price/date/platform/provider, or dead routes.
