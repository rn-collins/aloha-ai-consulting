## Decision

Decision Desk has a twelve-question coverage roadmap and one active flagship: Issue 01, prospective-client intake.

Issue 01 is governed by the [flagship completion contract](./ISSUE-01-FLAGSHIP-COMPLETION-CONTRACT.md). It may reach release readiness independently when that finite contract passes. Issues 02–12 are expansion cases and are not prerequisites for the first Decision Desk release, masterclass, clinic, or advisory demonstration.

The roadmap does not promise monthly publication, a twelve-issue inaugural bundle, or simultaneous R7 completion. Cadence and activation require later owner decisions informed by completed work, participant evidence, and demand.

## What the method should teach over time

Across completed cases, a participant should learn to:

1. decide whether a proposed AI use belongs in the work at all;
2. identify the decision owner, affected people, authoritative record, and nondelegable human judgment;
3. classify data, authority, consequence, reversibility, and escalation risk before selecting a product;
4. distinguish retrieval, summarization, generation, classification, recommendation, and autonomous action;
5. evaluate a named product and configuration rather than infer safety from a product category;
6. design a bounded predeployment test with representative inputs, error classes, stop rules, and accountable reviewers;
7. preserve notice, consent, contestability, accessibility, privacy, security, records, and professional obligations;
8. procure and contract for the actual data flow, vendors, subprocessors, retention, training use, access, and exit path;
9. monitor performance, incidents, workarounds, distributional effects, and model or policy drift; and
10. pause, roll back, replace, or retire a system when evidence no longer supports continued use.

These are coverage goals for the method, not simultaneous launch gates for Issue 01.

## Coverage method

A future case remains in the roadmap when it adds a materially different combination of work stage, AI role, consequence, evidence problem, decision owner, and reusable artifact. A case should be deferred when it duplicates completed learning, depends on individualized advice, is primarily a product tutorial, cannot yield a reusable decision tool, or cannot be responsibly bounded.

## Activation rule

A roadmap issue becomes active only through a written owner decision that records:

- the audience and bounded decision;
- why the issue is needed now;
- its completion contract and reusable output;
- required evidence and external dependencies;
- what work it displaces;
- the owner and target review date; and
- the release boundary.

Completing a matrix row, locating an additional authority, or reaching a calendar month does not activate an issue.

## Issue 02 freeze

The Issue 02 records remain preserved as valid expansion research at their stated limits. The remaining 44 recording-law rows, additional jurisdiction layers, product selection, testing, stakeholder work, and visual production are frozen. They may resume only when an activated Issue 02 contract, a real client scope, or another documented operational dependency establishes their necessity.

No existing Issue 02 record authorizes recording, transcription, product selection, a pilot, or legal clearance.

## Cross-cutting standards

Every activated issue must apply the relevant parts of the editorial research flywheel, including claim-level evidence, counterevidence, product/configuration specificity, affected-person evidence, visual integrity, rights, accessibility, testing, change monitoring, production QA, and owner approval. “Relevant” is governed by the issue's exact claims and delivery system; it does not mean exhausting every jurisdiction, sector, or product category.

## Deferred and excluded work

The roadmap does not itself activate:

- exhaustive sector-wide or nationwide surveys;
- model-versus-model comparisons without a bounded decision;
- individualized tool recommendations;
- unrelated product tutorials;
- a recurring subscription;
- accreditation, CLE, certification, or affiliation claims; or
- any live-data pilot.

## Reopening triggers

Reopen this coverage record if:

- a material AI role, affected group, lifecycle stage, or consequence is absent;
- two roadmap cases become duplicative;
- a case cannot be bounded to a supportable question and reusable tool;
- evidence requires splitting or combining cases;
- the audience or delivery model changes; or
- RN directs a different thesis, case, or order.
