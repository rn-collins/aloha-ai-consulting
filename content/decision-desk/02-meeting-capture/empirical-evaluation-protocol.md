# Preregistered meeting-capture evaluation protocol

Status: vendor-neutral protocol design. No product, participant recording, or outcome claim authorized.

## Decision question

For one frozen meeting class and product configuration, does capture improve the whole record-making process relative to an uncaptured human-led baseline without creating unacceptable legal, privacy, security, accessibility, accuracy, behavioral, relational, or records harm?

## Preconditions

Before testing a configured product, freeze the completed Permission and Record Map, authority packet, meeting-class specification, configuration dossier, synthetic scenario manifest, scoring guide, access/retention settings, stop authority, and analysis plan. Use fictional meetings first. Real people or organizational data require separate authority, privacy, security, labor, records, consent, and owner approvals.

## Conditions

- Human notes plus human-approved decision record; no recording.
- Bot transcript and summary plus required source-media review where authorized in synthetic testing.
- Bot output after a human-approved decision record is independently drafted.

Counterbalance order where feasible. Output graders should be blinded to product and condition when practicable.

## Required scenario strata

Include overlapping speech, interruption, cross-talk, silence, negation, disagreement, dissent, uncertain ownership, multiple dates, changing commitments, late arrival, weak audio, remote/in-room imbalance, accents, dialects, multilingual speech, domain vocabulary, disability/access needs, participant refusal, pause and restart, sensitive-topic emergence, prohibited-use instructions, permission changes, access leakage, export, correction, deletion, and retention expiry.

## Record-level outcomes

Score raw-media completeness where applicable, transcript accuracy, speaker attribution, generated-summary support, and decision-record fidelity separately. Record unsupported additions, omissions, distortions, wrong speakers, wrong owners, wrong decisions, date/deadline errors, erased ambiguity or dissent, false consensus, sensitive-data spill, and inaccessible correction paths.

A critical speaker, decision, owner, dissent, deadline, negation, permission, or access error is non-compensable; speed or readability elsewhere cannot cancel it.

## Human and system outcomes

Measure participant understanding, ability to refuse or pause, speaking distribution, withholding, side-channel migration, correction burden, source-review behavior, automation bias, manager reliance, accessibility burden, total cycle time, rework, complaints, unauthorized access, deletion success, retention expiry, incident containment, and total cost including review and remediation.

## Analysis and decision logic

- Preserve denominators and per-stratum results; do not report only an aggregate score.
- Separate transcription, summarization, and authoritative-record failures.
- Compare the whole process with the uncaptured baseline.
- Record missing data, exclusions, disagreement, deviations, incidents, and configuration changes.
- Do not tune on the sealed confirmation set.
- Any universal stop condition requires suspension and incident review; it cannot be waived after results are seen.

A passed synthetic evaluation authorizes, at most, consideration of a separately governed bounded pilot. It does not establish legal compliance, meaningful consent, product suitability, behavioral safety, or transferability to another meeting class, organization, jurisdiction, language, population, version, or configuration.
