# Before the Bot Joins the Meeting — masterclass specification

Status: production specification; not scheduled or offered
Length: 90 minutes
Version: MC-02-1.0

## Learning outcome

Participants can classify a meeting before capture, distinguish five records, map authority and meaningful choice, evaluate an exact proposal with fictional data, design objection/pause/correction/incident controls, and issue a bounded disposition without mistaking notice, consent, vendor claims, or generated summaries for proof.

## Required outputs

Governed decision; segment classification; people/power/access map; seven-layer authority gaps; five-record ownership table; vendor unknown list; objection/pause/correction/incident path; completed Permission and Record Map; bounded disposition and change conditions.

## Boundaries

Never request a real meeting, person, dispute, agreement, recording, transcript, vendor contract, credential, or confidential fact. Use Project Koa only. Interrupt legal-clearance or recording-permission requests. Do not collapse recording law into other layers, or automated captioning into an accommodation determination. The session itself is not recorded or transcribed.

## Misconceptions

Everyone clicked accept; the bot is visible; one-party consent ends analysis; enterprise means no training; transcript equals record; summary is only administrative; people can leave; indefinite retention is harmless; better notes automatically improve trust; accessibility requires the bot.

## Accessibility

Provide semantic HTML and plain text beforehand. Do not speed-score. Permit spoken, typed, or private responses and an untimed equivalent. Narrate visuals; do not rely on color; state breaks/transitions; handle accommodations separately from marketing consent.

## Required package

Slide deck with notes; workbook; fictional case; blank/completed MCPRM; answer key; facilitator guide; preparation guide; communications; resource guide; feedback/correction instrument; accessible-delivery manifest; rehearsal protocol. Naming a file does not complete it.

## Rehearsal gates

One internal timing rehearsal and one complete delivery rehearsal must record timing, confusion, access defects, overrun, misinformation risk, data leakage risk, contingencies, and critical/high defects. A script is not evidence that a rehearsal happened.

## Release boundary

No date, price, capacity, provider, certification, CLE, outcome, or availability is approved here. Commercial settings require an Issue 02 decision packet and RN approval.
