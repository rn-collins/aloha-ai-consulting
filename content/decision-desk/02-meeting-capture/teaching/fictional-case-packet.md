# Fictional case packet — Project Koa

Version: MC-FIC-1.0
All people, entities, locations, products, and facts are fictional. Do not substitute real meetings or confidential information.

## Proposal

Koa Works wants an AI assistant to join its weekly 60-minute “Launch Room.” It would create a transcript, summary, action list, and searchable institutional memory. Proposed rule: bot on unless the host turns it off.

## Participants

Hawaiʻi operations director and manager; California customer-research employee; Massachusetts product-security employee; New York contractor; Florida customer representative whose participation affects renewal; and a late-joining Pennsylvania employee. One participant requested real-time captions and a written decision record; the request does not authorize recording or establish that automated captions are sufficient.

## Agenda transitions

1. Routine milestones.
2. Customer feedback including an unnamed safety complaint.
3. Security vulnerability.
4. Personnel capacity and possible performance concern.
5. Renewal negotiation.
6. Open questions and assignments.

The host cannot pause by segment. Removing the bot ends automated captions. The late joiner sees the bot tile but no plain-language notice.

## Current records

The host keeps personal notes. Decisions reach a project board inconsistently. No owner reconciles transcript, summary, actions, and board.

## Proposed configuration

Vendor withheld; unprovided facts are unknown. Cloud transcription and summary; automatic project-board actions; project-channel transcript search; raw record retained 180 days; summary/actions indefinite. Training/reuse, subprocessors, support access, storage, backup deletion, and change notice are unknown. Corrections go to the host without deadline, audit trail, or downstream propagation. Calendar integration can join without new approval.

## Task

Classify each segment; identify power, voluntariness, access, and notice problems; separate five records; mark seven authority layers verified, unresolved, or not implicated; redesign before product selection; choose one bounded disposition; and record evidence that changes it.

## Do not infer

Do not assume one person’s consent resolves another location; visible bot presence is notice; accessibility requires recording; deletion exists; a summary is minutes; contractor/customer refusal is consequence-free; enterprise branding proves configuration; or partial recording layers resolve labor, privilege, privacy, records, access, sector, cross-border, or conflict questions.

## Expected range

The record cannot support a human pilot. A defensible answer is human notes only while redesigning classes/transitions, creating an equivalent non-capture path, resolving authority packets, defining the authoritative record, and testing a freezeable synthetic configuration. Do not capture is also supportable for the mixed meeting when reasons and change conditions are recorded.
