# Jurisdiction-specific authority packet method

Status: governed research design, version `MCAP-1.0`. The method is complete; no jurisdiction is cleared for participant recording. Not legal advice.

## Unit of analysis

Create a separate authority packet for each materially distinct combination of meeting class, participant locations, organization and worker status, sector, record owner, intended use, product data flow, storage location, and later disclosure. A headquarters address or host location is not a substitute for identifying every participant's physical location.

## Required layers

1. **Interception and recording.** Identify the federal baseline and every potentially applicable state, territorial, tribal, or foreign statute and controlling appellate interpretation. Record whether the communication is wire, oral, electronic, in person, remote, or mixed; whether an expectation-of-privacy element applies; whose consent is required; timing and form of notice; purpose/criminal-or-tortious-purpose exceptions; confidentiality restrictions; civil and criminal remedies; and conflict-of-law uncertainty.
2. **Workplace and labor.** Determine worker coverage, protected concerted activity, collective-bargaining duties, collective-agreement terms, employee-notice or monitoring statutes, retaliation risk, manager access, and whether refusal is meaningfully consequence-free. Do not treat statutory recording consent as labor authorization.
3. **Confidentiality, privilege, and professional duties.** Identify client, patient, student, customer, trade-secret, professional, contractual, common-interest, work-product, and privilege constraints. Consent to capture does not establish waiver, minimization, or permission for transcription, summarization, search, model processing, or disclosure.
4. **Records, discovery, and retention.** Classify each of the four artifacts under public-records, open-meetings, official-minutes, litigation-hold, discovery, evidentiary, records-schedule, deletion, and spoliation rules. Do not promise deletion where another duty requires preservation.
5. **Privacy, sector, and special-category data.** Evaluate comprehensive state privacy law, health, education, financial, communications, child, biometric/voiceprint, automated-decision, security, breach, data-broker, and contract restrictions for the actual data and use.
6. **Accessibility and language access.** Determine the affected person's communication needs and applicable accommodation/effective-communication duties. Automated captions or transcripts are not presumed accurate, sufficient, or an acceptable replacement for a requested auxiliary aid.
7. **Cross-border and localization.** Identify participant, controller, processor, subprocessor, storage, support-access, and transfer locations. Resolve lawful basis, transparency, processor terms, transfer mechanism, localization, data-subject rights, and onward transfers before capture.

## Source hierarchy

Use enacted/current statutory text, regulations, court rules, controlling appellate decisions, agency decisions or orders, and official government guidance. Secondary summaries may generate search terms but cannot close a row. Preserve URL, title, issuing body, effective or decision date, retrieval date, section/page/paragraph pinpoint, quoted proposition in bounded paraphrase, and later-history check.

## Research and review protocol

- Two researchers independently verify the recording-consent classification and citations for each jurisdiction.
- A qualified reviewer separately evaluates labor/employment, privilege/confidentiality, records, sector/privacy, accessibility, and cross-border layers implicated by the specific packet.
- Record conflicts, ambiguity, preemption, choice-of-law issues, proposed or delayed-effective law, and negative searches; do not force a binary label.
- Recheck all authorities immediately before any synthetic-to-human pilot decision and after any change in participants, location, meeting class, purpose, product, data flow, access, retention, or later use.
- A notice script, beep, click-through, calendar term, or bot presence is evidence to analyze, not self-proving consent.

## Deterministic disposition

`do_not_capture` if any controlling rule prohibits the proposed conduct or a presumptive no-capture class is not overcome. `human_notes_only` if any required layer is unresolved, reviewer authority is absent, choice is coercive, or a non-capture path is unavailable. `synthetic_test_eligible` requires all legal and organizational layers for the proposed synthetic test to pass, but does not authorize recording people. `bounded_pilot_for_separate_approval` can be considered only after the authority packet, frozen configuration, benchmark, affected-person process, and all six Permission and Record Map gates pass.

## Completion rule

A jurisdiction row is complete only when controlling recording authority and every implicated layer have current sources, pinpoints, reviewer, disposition, limitations, and next-review trigger. The matrix's existence and the federal/cross-border overlay register do not complete any jurisdiction row.
