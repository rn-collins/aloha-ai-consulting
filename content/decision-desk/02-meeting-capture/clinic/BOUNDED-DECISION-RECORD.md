# Clinic bounded decision record

Version: MCC-DECISION-1.0
This document is not legal advice, compliance clearance, procurement approval, product certification, or recording authorization.

## Participant scope

Record participant role category, authority confirmation, sanitized meeting-class label, high-level locations, purpose, current record, and requested decision. Do not record names or protected facts.

## Analysis

1. Segment map and presumptive no-capture transitions.
2. People, power, voluntariness, and equivalent alternative.
3. Seven authority layers: known, unresolved, not implicated, and reviewer needed.
4. Five records: purpose, owner, access, retention, correction, and authority.
5. Exact proposed functions and material data-flow unknowns.
6. Notice, objection, pause, exit, deletion, correction, and incident design.
7. Synthetic test requirements, thresholds, and stop rules.
8. Prohibited uses and non-negotiable conditions.

## Disposition

Select exactly one: do not capture; human notes only; redesign and synthetic test; bounded pilot for separate approval. State reasons, counterevidence considered, facts that would change the result, approvals still required, owner, and review trigger.

## Next actions

List no more than seven ordered actions, each with owner, evidence needed, stop condition, and due-date placeholder. “Ask counsel” must identify the finite authority question, not transfer the entire decision.

## Completed fictional example

Project Koa disposition: **human notes only**. Reasons: mixed sensitive segments; late joiner; no segment-level removal; no equivalent accessible non-capture path; seven incomplete authority layers across multiple locations; unknown vendor reuse/storage/deletion; overbroad access; no authoritative-record owner; and deficient correction propagation. Next actions: split meeting classes, designate human decision record, design equivalent captions and participation path, resolve finite authority packets, freeze product evidence, create synthetic tests, and rehearse pause/removal. No human pilot is authorized.

## Correction and sign-off

Participant receives the draft through the approved secure delivery path and may correct process facts within five business days. RN records accepted corrections and material disagreements. Sign-off confirms only that the record reflects the bounded clinic work.
