# Clinic follow-up, correction, privacy, incident, decline, and referral protocol

Version: MCC-CLOSE-1.0

## Follow-up package

Send only after attendance reconciliation: bounded decision record; blank MCPRM-1.0; issue resource guide; correction instructions; limitations; next-action summary; support contact; and optional advisory inquiry separated from marketing consent.

## Correction

Allow five business days for process-fact corrections. Record request, disposition, reason, version, owner, and downstream replacement. Do not erase a material professional disagreement by editing it into consensus.

## Data minimization and retention

Define separate schedules for application, reason codes, order/payment references, attendance, access requests, incident records, working notes, bounded deliverable, corrections, support, and optional consent. Delete working notes promptly after reconciled delivery unless an incident/hold requires preservation. Never promise deletion where law, chargeback, tax, security, or legal hold requires retention. RN must approve exact periods before launch.

## Incident response

Triggers include protected disclosure, unauthorized recording/transcription, uninvited attendee, access-control failure, misdirected file/message, exposed link, platform AI activation, or loss of deliverable integrity. Pause, contain, preserve minimum evidence, notify named owner, assess required notice, correct downstream copies, decide resume/cancel/refund, and record closure. Do not place sensitive detail in routine analytics or support tools.

## Decline language

“This clinic cannot responsibly address the request within its educational, nonconfidential scope. This is not a conclusion that the proposed recording is lawful or unlawful. Do not send additional facts or documents. The next appropriate step is [finite category of qualified counsel, labor representative, accessibility specialist, security/privacy owner, or records authority].”

## Referral

Give role/category and finite question, not an endorsement or guarantee. Do not transmit participant information without separate permission.

## Closure

Close only after deliverable delivery, correction window, refunds if any, incident disposition, deletion/retention actions, and participant notice reconcile. Optional feedback and advisory inquiry remain separate.
