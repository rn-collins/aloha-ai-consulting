# Clinic participation acknowledgments and no-recording protocol

Version: MCC-PART-1.0

## Required acknowledgments

Participant confirms authority to discuss a sanitized workflow; will not disclose protected or identifying information; understands the service is educational workflow analysis, not legal advice or authorization; understands no product is certified; will not implement solely from the clinic; and accepts the correction/retention process.

Required acknowledgment is separate from optional marketing consent. Silence, attendance, or payment is not consent to marketing, research, quotation, photography, or recording.

## Clinic recording rule

The clinic is not audio/video recorded, transcribed, summarized by a meeting bot, or used to train/evaluate a model. Provider recording, transcript, AI companion, and automatic notes must be disabled and checked before admission. If a platform feature cannot be disabled reliably, use the documented fallback platform or cancel/refund.

## Participant control

Participants may pause, take a private break, decline to answer, use chat or speech, request restatement, or leave without explaining. Leaving does not authorize retention of unfinished workflow content. Accessibility requests are handled through a data-minimizing path.

## Interruption script

“Please stop there. That sounds like it may identify a person, matter, dispute, or protected record. I do not need that information. We will remove it from the working notes, restate only the workflow structure if safe, and pause or end the clinic if it cannot be sanitized.”

## Optional permissions

Any later permission to quote feedback, use an anonymized example, photograph a setting, or contact about services requires a separate, specific, revocable record. Declining has no effect on clinic access or quality.

## Incident boundary

Unauthorized platform recording, accidental disclosure, uninvited attendee, link compromise, or inability to disable capture triggers an immediate pause and incident hold. The substantive session does not resume until the named owner records a safe disposition.
