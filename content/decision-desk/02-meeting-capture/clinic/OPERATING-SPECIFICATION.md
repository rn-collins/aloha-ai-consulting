# Meeting Capture Decision Desk Clinic — operating specification

Status: internal draft; inactive
Version: MCC-1.0
Format assumption: four participants, two hours, one nonconfidential meeting class each, RN facilitator, named support operator required before rehearsal.

## Outcome

Each participant receives a Meeting Capture Permission and Record Map and next-action record with one disposition: do not capture, human notes only, redesign and synthetic test, or bounded pilot for separate approval. The clinic never authorizes recording.

## Eligible

Authorized meeting owner, operations lead, counsel/compliance partner, records/security owner, worker-side policy representative, accessibility/language-access owner, or implementation adviser able to discuss structure without identifying people or disclosing protected facts.

## Excluded or declined

Covert capture; active dispute, investigation, discipline, grievance, bargaining, or legal strategy; individual employee/client/patient/student/customer facts; real recordings/transcripts; credentials or product access; biometric identification; unresolved participant locations; legal advice; nationwide clearance; live testing; product certification; or pressure to approve a preset deployment.

## Minimum workflow

1. Reconfirm data boundary and authority.
2. Classify one meeting and its segments.
3. Map roles, locations, voluntariness, leverage, and access.
4. Map seven authority layers without resolving legal questions in session.
5. Separate five record classes and owners.
6. Trace proposed capture/data flow from public or participant-prepared nonconfidential facts.
7. Design notice, equivalent alternative, pause, exit, correction, deletion, and incident paths.
8. Complete the bounded disposition and evidence gaps.
9. Read back limitations and next actions.

## Stop conditions

Stop substantive work if the participant discloses protected facts, seeks human recording permission, cannot establish authority to discuss the workflow, cannot avoid identifying people, reveals an active dispute/investigation, asks RN to decide law, or lacks a safe non-capture alternative. Quarantine submitted content, follow the incident protocol, and provide decline/referral language.

## Records

Retain only the minimum administrative order record and participant-approved bounded deliverable under the approved schedule. Do not record/transcribe the clinic. Do not place workflow narratives in checkout, email subject lines, calendars, analytics, or support metadata.

## Completion

Attendance is not completion. Completion requires delivered bounded record, participant correction window, closure of any incident hold, and explicit notice that no implementation or legal authorization was granted.
