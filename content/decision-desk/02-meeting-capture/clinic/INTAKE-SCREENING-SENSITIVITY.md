# Clinic intake, screening, and sensitivity protocol

Version: MCC-INTAKE-1.0

## Minimal intake fields

Role/authority category; organization type; one meeting-class label; participant role types; high-level jurisdictions/locations; required/optional attendance; agenda segment classes; current authoritative record; proposed bot functions; exact vendor/configuration if publicly identifiable; proposed access/retention; non-capture alternative; objection/pause/correction route; desired clinic decision; access needs.

## Never request

Names, emails beyond administrative contact, recordings, transcripts, meeting links, invitations, agendas, minutes, contracts, collective agreements, complaints, medical/disability details, case facts, customer or employee records, credentials, vendor portals, proprietary documents, or raw sensitive narratives.

## Screen

- authority to discuss process;
- ability to remove identifiers;
- active dispute/investigation or legal deadline;
- covert or nonconsensual intent;
- participant-location uncertainty;
- privileged, employment, labor, health, education, financial, child, safety, security, or community sensitivity;
- inability to offer equivalent participation without capture;
- accessibility support needed for the clinic;
- conflict or independence concern;
- request exceeding education/workflow design.

## States

Eligible; clarification required; deferred pending sanitized restatement; declined; incident hold; or capacity waitlist. Only eligible may proceed to payment. Clarification requests repeat the no-sensitive-data rule and never solicit the missing protected facts.

## Human decision

RN records state, bounded reason code, version, date, and next step. No automated tool accepts or rejects clinic applications. A decline does not state a legal conclusion.

## Accidental disclosure

Stop review, do not copy the content into other systems, restrict access, record only an incident ID and category, follow approved deletion/retention constraints, notify the privacy/incident owner, and send a neutral resubmission instruction if appropriate.
