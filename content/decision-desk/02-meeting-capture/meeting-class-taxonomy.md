# Meeting-class taxonomy and classification protocol

Status: vendor-neutral design record; classifications require organization-specific review. No class is cleared for capture.

## Classification rule

Classify the work before inviting or configuring a bot. Use the highest-risk applicable flag; do not average risk downward because a meeting is routine, recurring, or attended by senior people.

## Required dimensions

| Dimension | Record |
|---|---|
| Purpose | decision, coordination, learning, deliberation, evaluation, care, negotiation, complaint, investigation, or other |
| Consequence | informational, relational, employment, financial, legal, health, safety, security, rights, or cultural |
| People | employees, contractors, clients, counsel, community members, minors, vulnerable people, guests, or bystanders |
| Power | who requests capture; who can refuse; consequence of refusing; who controls access and employment/opportunity outcomes |
| Sensitivity | privilege, personnel, health, accommodation, protected activity, trade secret, security, complaint, cultural knowledge, or special-category data |
| Jurisdiction | physical location of each participant, organization, system, storage, and intended later use |
| Record purpose | why raw media, transcript, summary, or decision record is necessary separately |
| Alternatives | no capture, human notes, rotating note-taker, structured decision log, or delayed participant-approved record |

## Starting classes—not approvals

| Class | Default posture | Required challenge |
|---|---|---|
| Routine coordination | uncaptured until purpose and alternatives are documented | Why is a searchable verbatim record necessary rather than a decision log? |
| Formal governance or board action | human-approved authoritative minutes; capture separately reviewed | What law, governing document, records rule, and approval process controls? |
| Training or demonstration | synthetic or staged material preferred | Can the objective be met without recording real participants? |
| Customer or client conversation | uncaptured until authority, confidentiality, notice, and later use are resolved | Would capture change the relationship or collect unnecessary facts? |
| Personnel, performance, discipline, or accommodation | presumptive no capture | Could the record become unreviewed personnel evidence or suppress candor? |
| Legal, privileged, investigative, or complaint work | presumptive no capture | Who can waive, discover, access, retain, or compel the record? |
| Labor organizing or protected concerted activity | presumptive no capture | Are labor rights, collective agreements, monitoring limits, and coercion resolved? |
| Health, safety, security, or emergency | presumptive no capture unless separately necessary and authorized | Does capture create new exposure, delay, or access risk? |
| Community, cultural, restorative, or relationship-repair work | presumptive no capture | Who owns the knowledge and can meaningfully govern reuse? |
| Negotiation or confidential strategy | presumptive no capture | Does capture undermine the function or create a harmful discoverable repository? |

## Reclassification triggers

Reclassify immediately when a participant, purpose, topic, jurisdiction, data category, bot, integration, access role, or intended later use changes. A meeting that begins as routine does not retain clearance after sensitive content emerges. The operating design must permit immediate pause and an uncaptured continuation.
