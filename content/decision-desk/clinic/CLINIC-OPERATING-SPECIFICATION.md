# Issue 01 bounded clinic — operating specification

Status: draft operating record; inactive; not enrollment or delivery authorization  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: RN Collins / Aloha AI  
Source case: Issue 01 — AI-assisted prospective-client intake

## Purpose

The clinic is a facilitated application of the Decision Desk method to one nonconfidential workflow supplied by each participant. It helps a participant frame a decision, distinguish authoritative records from summaries and claims, identify evidence gaps, and leave with a bounded decision record and next actions.

It is educational and operational. It does not provide legal advice, create an attorney-client relationship, certify a product, approve procurement, clear a jurisdiction, or authorize live sensitive-data processing.

## Delivery boundary

- Format: one two-hour facilitated small-group clinic.
- Capacity: four participants maximum unless a later staffing record proves additional capacity.
- Unit of work: one workflow and one decision question per participant.
- Inputs: redacted or fictional descriptions only; no client, patient, employee, student, applicant, or other identifiable records.
- Output: a bounded decision record, evidence-gap list, safeguards, stop conditions, and next-action plan.
- Default disposition where evidence is insufficient: continue human-led, redesign, or obtain qualified review.
- No product testing, account access, system configuration, data migration, legal opinion, or implementation is included.

## Eligibility

A participant is eligible only when all are true:

1. They have authority to discuss the workflow at the level proposed.
2. They can describe it without confidential, privileged, regulated, proprietary, or identifying information.
3. The question can be bounded to one workflow and one decision.
4. They understand that the clinic produces an educational decision record, not clearance.
5. They accept the participation, privacy, conduct, and correction terms.
6. Their access needs can be met for the scheduled format or an agreed alternative.

Good-fit questions include whether to investigate, reject, redesign, or separately evaluate an AI-assisted step; what evidence would be needed; where authoritative records must remain; and which human review or stop rules belong in the workflow.

## Exclusions

Do not enroll or continue a case involving:

- an active emergency, filing deadline, limitation period, crisis, or urgent rights determination;
- a request for individualized legal, medical, employment, financial, immigration, benefits, or security advice;
- live or identifiable sensitive records;
- facts whose disclosure may waive privilege or breach confidentiality;
- covert surveillance, deception, discrimination, evasion, weapons, exploitation, or unlawful activity;
- adjudication of an individual, disciplinary action, eligibility decision, or other high-impact determination;
- a request to endorse a vendor, approve procurement, or authorize deployment;
- litigation strategy, active incident response, or forensic investigation;
- a scope too broad to produce an honest result within the clinic.

The facilitator may pause or stop any case when new information triggers an exclusion.

## Clinic sequence

| Time | Activity | Record produced |
|---|---|---|
| Before session | Fit and sensitivity screen | accept, clarify, defer, or decline |
| 0–15 min | Bound the decision and restate limits | decision statement |
| 15–40 min | Map workflow and authoritative record | current-state map |
| 40–65 min | Identify people, data, harms, and dependencies | risk/evidence map |
| 65–90 min | Evaluate proposal and evidence quality | diligence gaps |
| 90–110 min | Select disposition and safeguards | bounded decision |
| 110–120 min | Confirm ownership, dates, and correction route | next-action record |

## Staffing and capacity gate

The clinic may be offered only when one facilitator can deliver the published scope, support the accessibility plan, review four screens before the session, return records within the stated service level, and operate the interruption protocol. If that capacity is not evidenced, reduce seats or keep booking inactive.

## Release boundary

This specification does not activate dates, price, enrollment, payment, email, scheduling, or delivery. Those remain blocked until end-to-end tests and RN approval.
