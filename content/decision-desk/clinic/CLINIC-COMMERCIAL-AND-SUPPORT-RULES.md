# Clinic commercial, scheduling, cancellation, refund, and support rules

Status: draft policy; inactive until price, dates, systems, and owner are approved  
Version: 0.1

## Information required before purchase

A booking surface must display, before payment:

- exact date, start/end time, and timezone;
- remote or physical delivery method and access requirements;
- maximum capacity;
- total price and taxes/fees, if any;
- what is included and excluded;
- preparation deadline;
- cancellation, transfer, refund, no-show, reschedule, and organizer-cancellation rules;
- privacy notice and data-retention summary;
- accessibility-request route and response timeframe;
- support contact and response timeframe;
- statement that the clinic is not legal advice and creates no attorney-client relationship;
- statement that recording is prohibited for the initial release;
- correction and complaint routes.

No price or date is approved by this record.

## Capacity and scheduling

- Default maximum: four participants.
- A seat is confirmed only after fit screening, payment, and confirmation delivery all succeed.
- Maintain a waitlist only with affirmative consent.
- Never oversell capacity.
- Publish a registration close time that allows screening and access preparation.
- If fewer participants improve privacy or access, the facilitator may reduce capacity without changing the promised individual scope.
- A participant may request a reasonable format alternative; unresolved access needs must be handled before the nonrefundable deadline.

## Draft cancellation and refund logic

These terms require owner and payment-provider review before activation:

- Participant cancellation before the published full-refund deadline: full refund.
- Participant cancellation after that deadline but before the transfer deadline: seat transfer or credit, subject to fit screening.
- Late cancellation or no-show: no automatic refund, except documented access failure, emergency exception, or applicable law/policy.
- Organizer cancellation: full refund, plus a reschedule option that requires affirmative acceptance.
- Material organizer change to date, format, scope, or facilitator: participant may accept the change or receive a full refund.
- Duplicate charge or technical payment error: prompt correction and refund.
- Declined fit screen after payment: full refund.
- Session stopped because the participant knowingly violates confidentiality/safety rules: disposition reviewed individually; do not promise an automatic refund.

Refund timing, method, fees, taxes, currency, credit expiration, and governing consumer requirements must match the selected payment system and published terms.

## Support service levels

Before activation, assign real owners and test these targets:

- accessibility request acknowledgment: one business day;
- fit-screen clarification: two business days;
- payment or confirmation failure: one business day;
- urgent day-of-session access issue: monitored from 30 minutes before through session close;
- deliverable correction acknowledgment: two business days;
- final bounded decision record: five business days after the clinic, unless a shorter published period is actually supportable.

## Failure handling

- Payment succeeds but confirmation fails: hold the seat, notify manually, and log the defect.
- Confirmation succeeds but calendar/access link fails: issue corrected details through the verified contact channel.
- Platform outage: use the rehearsed backup or reschedule; do not improvise with an inaccessible or unapproved platform.
- Facilitator unavailable: offer replacement only if equally qualified and disclosed; otherwise reschedule or refund.
- Participant cannot safely discuss the case: switch to the fictional case, defer, or decline/refund according to the published rule.

## Activation gate

No checkout, scheduling, automated email, or enrollment may activate until test transactions, receipts, refunds, calendar behavior, timezone rendering, communications, privacy handling, accessibility requests, support escalation, and organizer cancellation pass end-to-end QA.
