# Clinic intake, sensitivity screen, and informed participation record

Status: draft; do not collect responses until the approved form and privacy notice are active  
Version: 0.1

## Data-minimizing intake

Collect only:

- participant name and contact details;
- organization and role, if relevant;
- access and communication needs;
- a one-sentence, nonconfidential workflow description;
- the decision the participant wants to make;
- whether they have authority to discuss the workflow;
- acknowledgments and consent selections.

Do not request names of affected people, case numbers, matter facts, documents, recordings, screenshots, credentials, health information, financial account information, government identifiers, or verbatim confidential content.

## Required pre-screen questions

1. Can you describe the workflow without identifying any person?
2. Does the description contain privileged, confidential, regulated, contractual, or trade-secret information?
3. Is there an emergency, active dispute, deadline, investigation, incident, or individual high-impact decision?
4. Are you seeking legal or other regulated professional advice?
5. Do you control or have permission to discuss this workflow?
6. Can the question be reduced to one workflow and one bounded decision?
7. What access format or participation support would help you take part?

Any “yes” to questions 2–4, “no” to question 5, or uncertainty requires human review before acceptance.

## Screen dispositions

- **Accept:** clearly within scope and safe to discuss.
- **Clarify:** one bounded follow-up can resolve fit without collecting sensitive facts.
- **Defer:** potentially appropriate after redaction, authority confirmation, or qualified advice.
- **Decline/referral:** excluded or cannot be made safe within clinic constraints.

A screen is not an evaluation of the merits of the participant’s underlying matter.

## Informed participation acknowledgments

The participant must affirm each statement separately:

- I will use only fictional, abstracted, or properly redacted information.
- I will not share privileged, confidential, regulated, or identifying information.
- I understand the clinic is educational and operational, not legal advice.
- I understand no attorney-client, fiduciary, consultant-client, certification, procurement, or implementation relationship is created by attendance alone.
- I understand no product or workflow is approved for live sensitive data.
- I understand other participants may hear my contributions in a group session.
- I will not disclose another participant’s information outside the session.
- I understand confidentiality among participants is requested but cannot be guaranteed.
- I may pause, skip an exercise, request an alternative, or withdraw.
- I understand the facilitator may interrupt or stop discussion to protect people or information.

## Optional permissions

Each must default to off and be independently selectable:

- permission to retain de-identified feedback;
- permission to contact the participant about advisory services;
- permission to use an anonymized quotation;
- permission to record the session.

The initial clinic release prohibits recording, so the recording option must remain absent or disabled unless a later, separately approved protocol replaces that rule.

## Interruption protocol

If sensitive information appears:

1. Stop the speaker and do not repeat the content.
2. Ask the group not to copy, save, or redistribute it.
3. Exclude the detail from notes and outputs.
4. Remove it from any editable draft where technically possible.
5. Decide whether the case can continue using an abstract substitute.
6. Log the interruption without reproducing the sensitive content.
7. Escalate only through the published privacy/security path when necessary.

Consent is documented before participation and re-confirmed orally at the opening boundary statement.
