# Clinic decline, referral, escalation, and quality gates

Status: draft control record  
Version: 0.1

## Decline principles

Decline promptly, respectfully, and without unnecessary fact collection when the request falls outside the published boundary. A decline does not state that the underlying idea is unlawful, unsafe, meritless, or impossible. It states only that this clinic cannot responsibly address it within its scope, staffing, evidence, or format.

## Decline reasons

Use one or more neutral categories:

- urgent or deadline-driven matter;
- individualized professional advice required;
- confidential, privileged, regulated, or identifying information cannot be removed;
- authority to discuss the workflow is absent or unclear;
- active dispute, investigation, incident, or high-impact individual decision;
- request seeks product endorsement, procurement approval, implementation, or live-data authorization;
- harmful, unlawful, deceptive, or discriminatory objective;
- access need cannot yet be met in the scheduled format;
- scope cannot be bounded to one workflow and decision;
- actual or reasonably perceived conflict;
- capacity unavailable.

Do not reproduce sensitive facts in the decline record.

## Referral pathways

Where useful, provide a category—not an unverified endorsement:

- licensed counsel in the relevant jurisdiction and subject area;
- privacy, cybersecurity, accessibility, employment, procurement, or records professional;
- emergency, crisis, court, regulator, benefits, or incident-response channel;
- vendor security/privacy documentation or organizational procurement owner;
- a later clinic date or fictional-case masterclass when the barrier is scope or capacity.

Verify any named referral’s role, availability, jurisdiction, and permission before presenting it as available. State whether Aloha AI receives any compensation or has a relationship. Never promise that a third party will accept the matter.

## Conflict and sensitivity escalation

A potential conflict or sensitivity concern goes to a designated human reviewer. Until resolved:

- do not circulate the intake;
- do not place the participant in a group;
- do not request additional merits facts;
- do not imply acceptance;
- preserve only the minimum administrative record;
- refund any payment if the person cannot be accepted.

## Participant-facing decline template

Subject: Your Aloha AI clinic request

Thank you for sharing a bounded description of the workflow. Based on the information provided, this clinic is not the right setting for the request because [neutral category]. This is not a judgment about the merits of the underlying matter.

We have [issued/initiated] the applicable refund. If useful, the appropriate next category of support may be [verified category or resource]. Please do not send additional confidential or identifying details in reply. Questions about the screening decision, accessibility, or refund can be sent through [approved support channel].

## Clinic quality gates

The clinic package is complete as a draft only when:

- operating specification, intake/sensitivity screen, consent, decision record, commercial rules, follow-up, retention, decline, and referral records agree;
- all participant claims match actual staffing and capacity;
- price/date/timezone/platform owners are filled before activation;
- privacy and terms review is dispositioned;
- accessibility formats and support route are operationally tested;
- screen, payment, refund, receipt, calendar, confirmation, reminder, cancellation, deliverable, correction, complaint, and incident flows pass;
- a complete rehearsal includes an excluded case, sensitive disclosure interruption, access request, payment failure, organizer cancellation, and correction request;
- critical and high defects are closed;
- RN explicitly approves activation.

Until those gates pass, the clinic remains documented but unavailable for booking.
