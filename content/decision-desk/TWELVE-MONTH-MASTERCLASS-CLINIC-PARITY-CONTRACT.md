# Twelve-month masterclass and clinic parity contract

Status: governing internal production contract; draft branch only; not a release authorization  
Owner: RN Collins / Aloha AI  
Program window: September 2026–August 2027  
Adopted from owner direction: 2026-08-08  
Applies to: Decision Desk Issues 01–12

## Owner direction

Every month begins with one free Decision Desk masterclass in week 1 and continues with the same accompanying US$295-per-participant, bounded clinic in every remaining week: three clinic cohorts in a four-week month or four clinic cohorts in a five-week month. Issues 02–12 must receive the same depth, diligence, evidentiary discipline, operational design, participant protection, and release scrutiny applied to Issue 01.

The annual program is not satisfied by eleven topic outlines, renamed Issue 01 templates, generic clinic copy, or a calendar. Each month must become its own coherent article, teaching, clinic, commercial, visual, accessibility, and operational release unit.

## Meaning of parity

Parity means equivalent acceptance coverage and rigor, not mechanically identical word counts or file counts.

Shared infrastructure may be inherited only where the underlying rule truly remains the same. Every inherited element must be reviewed, versioned, and either affirmed or overridden for the new issue. Month-specific research, claims, authorities, affected people, fictional scenario, decision instrument, clinic scope, exclusions, risks, visuals, rehearsals, and release decision may not be copied without independent substantiation.

A month passes parity only when a reviewer can trace:

1. the governed question to the evidence;
2. the evidence to the article and Source Desk;
3. the article to the masterclass learning design;
4. the masterclass instrument to a safe fictional exercise;
5. the clinic promise to a bounded real participant workflow;
6. every public and commercial promise to actual operational capacity;
7. every release claim to observed evidence; and
8. every limitation or unperformed dependency to an honest disposition.

## Required paired release unit for each issue

Each Issue 02–12 dossier must contain all of the following.

### A. Issue activation and completion contract

- bounded question, audience, use context, jurisdictions/sectors, and affected people;
- explicit inclusions, exclusions, prohibited uses, displacement decision, and change-control rule;
- month-specific deliverables and acceptance criteria;
- research, editorial, teaching, clinic, operations, accessibility, support, and approval owners;
- finite external dependencies and fallbacks;
- accurate readiness state and release boundary.

### B. Research and evidence system

- research protocol and source-class hierarchy;
- claim ledger, source ledger, pinpoints, search log, contradictions, limitations, and freshness dates;
- controlling authority separated from guidance, commentary, allegation, vendor statement, observed result, RN analysis, recommendation, and unknown;
- jurisdiction/sector boundaries appropriate to the fictional and clinic contexts;
- affected-person and disparate-failure evidence;
- exact product/configuration evidence where relevant;
- documented no-product/no-test disposition where lawful access or sufficient evidence is unavailable;
- independent-review questions narrowed to finite propositions;
- public Source Desk that supports the article without exposing sealed exercises or sensitive facts.

### C. Editorial and visual release candidate

- complete Decision Desk article answering the governed question;
- line-by-line factual, attribution, contradiction, limitation, and voice reconciliation;
- evidentiary visual story plan;
- original month-specific System X-Ray;
- rights-reviewed primary-document or documentary authority image when supportable;
- real-environment photography brief, release/permission record, and omission-ready fallback;
- captions, detailed alt text, credits, sources, dates, rights bases, manipulation disclosures, and claim mappings;
- no decorative generic AI imagery and no fabricated reporting.

### D. Reusable decision instrument

- blank participant version;
- completed fictional example;
- scoring or disposition logic;
- authority, purpose, people, data, workflow, exact configuration, evidence gaps, safeguards, escalation, monitoring, correction, stop authority, review date, and next-action fields as relevant;
- bounded outcomes that cannot be mistaken for legal clearance, product certification, procurement approval, or outcome guarantee;
- accessible semantic formats.

### E. Complete 90-minute masterclass

- month-specific learning objectives and timed run of show;
- slide deck;
- participant workbook;
- wholly fictional/nonconfidential case packet;
- completed example;
- answer key and scoring notes;
- facilitator guide with exact timing, prompts, likely misconceptions, access notes, breaks/alternatives, and contingency instructions;
- preparation, confirmation, reminder, follow-up, correction, and support communications;
- no request that a participant bring confidential, personal, client, employee, patient, student, customer, or proprietary facts;
- two complete rehearsals for a new issue, with observed defects and remediation recorded.

### F. Complete accompanying clinic

Every month requires one governed US$295-per-participant clinic product that applies that month’s decision method to one bounded, nonconfidential participant workflow. That same clinic is delivered to a separate small cohort in every week remaining after the free week-1 masterclass: three cohorts in a four-week month or four cohorts in a five-week month. A clinic may not be silently omitted. If a proposed topic cannot support a safe clinic, the issue must be redesigned, deferred, replaced, or left dark.

Repetition does not permit scope drift. The core promise, instrument, eligibility, exclusions, privacy boundary, capacity, and materials remain constant across the month unless a controlled correction is required. Each cohort must have separate screening, payment, confirmation, attendance, support, incident, follow-up, refund, and reconciliation records.

The clinic package must include:

- clinic title, purpose, audience, promise, deliverable, duration, capacity, staffing, and month-specific value rationale;
- eligibility, exclusions, conflict/sensitivity screen, prohibited facts/data, and decline/referral paths;
- minimal intake and human screening;
- required acknowledgments separated from optional permissions and marketing consent;
- interruption, objection, withdrawal, accommodation, and no-recording rules;
- month-specific bounded decision-record template and completed fictional example;
- preparation and facilitation plan;
- scheduling, timezone, attendance, cancellation, refund, transfer, no-show, and organizer-failure rules;
- support, correction, incident, privacy, retention/deletion, and follow-up protocols;
- post-payment decline and duplicate-charge recovery;
- no live system authorization, legal/professional advice, certification, procurement approval, or sensitive-data processing;
- complete clinic rehearsal and defect-remediation record;
- a cohort schedule for every remaining week of the month, with per-cohort capacity and operational ownership; and
- a between-cohort correction rule that permits evidence, accessibility, safety, and defect fixes without silently changing the offer.

### G. Commercial and communications journey

- distinct article, masterclass, clinic, and advisory roles;
- month-specific titles, dates, prices, capacity, minimum viable cohort, scholarship policy, delivery method, inclusions, exclusions, and value/unit-economics review;
- pre-purchase terms, privacy, accessibility-request, support, cancellation, and refund surfaces;

### H. Accessibility and delivery formats

- heading, landmark, label, fieldset, live-region, table, link, keyboard, focus, contrast, image-alternative, caption, zoom/reflow, and responsive checks;

### I. QA, readiness, and release decision

- month-specific factual/editorial/legal-boundary review;
- contradiction and gap audit across all public and internal records;
- visual-rights and permission disposition;
- at least 24 end-to-end commercial scenarios, expanded where the issue requires more;
- masterclass rehearsal, clinic rehearsal, correction pass, support/incident rehearsal, and rollback plan;

## Readiness vocabulary

The following terms must be used precisely:

- **Planned:** the governed question and production slot exist.
- **Activated:** the month-specific completion contract and research protocol are approved for draft work.
- **Drafted:** the required artifact exists but has not passed all checks.
- **Technically checked:** specified automated or structural checks were actually run and recorded.
- **Observed:** a person or functioning tool actually exercised the rendered behavior described.
- **Rehearsed:** the complete timed session or clinic was performed against its runbook and defects were logged.
- **Approved:** RN explicitly accepted the named decision or release candidate.
- **Release-ready:** all month-specific gates passed within recorded scopes.
- **Released:** the approved production action actually occurred.

Future work may be planned, drafted, or technically checked now. It may not be labeled observed, rehearsed, approved, release-ready, or released unless the corresponding event occurred.

## Work that can be completed in advance

The program may complete substantial future-month work now, including research, evidence mapping, articles, Source Desks, instruments, fictional cases, decks, workbooks, facilitator guides, clinic specifications, intake and consent records, visitor journeys, commercial state models, communications, visual plans, original diagrams, accessible exports, test fixtures, QA scripts, decision packets, and omission fallbacks.

The following require real events or access and must retain truthful status until then:

- a timed rehearsal;
- human or representative-user accessibility validation;
- browser/assistive-technology observation that the available environment cannot execute;
- a real environmental photograph;
- third-party permission, legal review, vendor response, or provider approval;
- test transactions or messages using configured providers;
- named day-of operator acceptance;
- RN approval; and
- merge, scheduling, sale, publication, sending, or release.

An unavailable external event does not excuse incomplete preparatory work. It resolves to a finite fallback, narrowed claim, recorded blocker, defer/replace decision, or dark month.

## Anti-shortcut tests

A month fails parity if any of the following is true:

- only its title, outline, calendar entry, or research question exists;
- Issue 01 language was copied without month-specific verification;
- the clinic is generic or not tied to the month’s instrument;
- authorities or vendor claims are cited without claim-level support and freshness;
- fictional exercises reuse sensitive or plausibly identifying facts;
- public promises exceed staffing, time, evidence, or operational capacity;
- visuals are decorative, unlicensed, generated as reporting, or missing rights records;
- accessibility is asserted from file creation alone;
- rehearsal is asserted from reading a script;
- approval is inferred from planning authorization;
- automated checks are described as human validation; or
- unresolved high/critical defects are relabeled as acceptable limitations.
