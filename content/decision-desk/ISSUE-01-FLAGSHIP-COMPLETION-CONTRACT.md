# Issue 01 flagship completion contract

Status: governing production contract; active; not a release authorization  
Owner: RN Collins / Aloha AI  
Adopted: 2026-08-08  
Flagship question: Should a small law firm use AI to summarize prospective-client intake?

## Purpose

Issue 01 is the first complete proof of the Aloha AI Decision Desk method and the source case for the first masterclass and bounded clinic. The release unit is one coherent system, not a collection of unfinished research files.

Issues 02–12 are an expansion roadmap. They are not prerequisites for Issue 01, the first masterclass, the bounded clinic, or the first Decision Desk release.

## Audience and decision

The primary audience is a small-law-firm owner, operations lead, supervising lawyer, legal technologist, or implementation adviser deciding whether and how AI-assisted intake summarization belongs in a specific workflow.

The flagship does not promise legal advice, a nationwide product endorsement, a universal safe configuration, or permission to use live prospective-client data. It teaches and demonstrates a reproducible decision method.

## Release unit

Issue 01 is complete only when all nine deliverables below satisfy their acceptance criteria.

### 1. Decision Desk article

Acceptance criteria:

- one release-ready, photojournalistic article answers the governed question;
- the finding distinguishes sourced fact, authority, vendor statement, observed result, RN analysis, recommendation, and unknown;
- every material claim maps to the claim ledger and a current source;
- the article accurately reports a documented no-product finding if no candidate qualifies;
- the headline, dek, body, visuals, Source Desk, tool, and teaching materials do not contradict one another.

### 2. Auditable Source Desk

Acceptance criteria:

- claim, source, pinpoint, search, contradiction, limitation, and freshness records reconcile;
- controlling authority is distinguished from guidance, commentary, allegations, and vendor claims;
- unsupported propositions are removed, narrowed, attributed, or labeled unknown;
- link and citation integrity passes;
- the public Source Desk discloses material limits without exposing sealed benchmark answers or sensitive records.

### 3. Intake Summarization Evaluation Card

Acceptance criteria:

- the card records purpose, jurisdiction, matter scope, prohibited uses, authoritative record, data lifecycle, named configuration, representative tests, reviewers, thresholds, stop authority, incident path, review date, and disposition;
- outcomes are limited to reject, redesign and retest, continue human-led, or authorize a separately governed bounded pilot;
- the card never outputs legal clearance or a product-wide endorsement;
- a completed teaching example and a reusable blank version exist in accessible formats.

### 4. Product-evidence demonstration

Acceptance criteria:

- one exact product/configuration is tested only if sufficient evidence and lawful access permit a freezeable synthetic evaluation;
- otherwise the release contains a transparent no-product finding and demonstrates the diligence and test design without invented results;
- no live, privileged, confidential, personal, or prospective-client data is used;
- observed results, vendor claims, and untested assumptions remain visibly separate.

External vendor silence expires as a dependency on the response deadline recorded in the production tracker. The fallback is the no-product finding; silence cannot keep the flagship open indefinitely.

### 5. Sixty-minute masterclass

Learning outcome: participants can redesign an intake workflow, determine what evidence is authoritative, evaluate an exact AI proposal, apply the Evaluation Card, and reach a bounded disposition without mistaking marketing claims or model output for proof.

Run of show:

| Time | Segment | Participant output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7 min | Private opening view; bound the intake decision | Initial decision statement |
| 7–16 min | Prospective-client duties, data boundaries, and jurisdiction | Risk-and-authority map |
| 16–28 min | Actual workflow, exceptions, handoffs, and authoritative source | Redrawn intake flow |
| 28–37 min | Product/configuration evidence and no-product finding | Diligence gaps |
| 37–49 min | Synthetic case and disconfirming-evidence exercise | Scored Evaluation Card |
| 49–57 min | Human review, dissent, accessibility, if–then action, and stop rules | Safeguard/maintenance set |
| 57–60 min | Disposition, owner, and review trigger | Bounded decision |

The masterclass follows the participatory standard. Participants do structured work on supplied fictional facts; RN teaches, challenges, and synthesizes rather than privately delivering an answer.

Required teaching assets:

- slide deck;
- participant workbook;
- case packet using fictional data;
- completed example;
- answer key and scoring notes;
- facilitator guide with timing, prompts, misconceptions, access notes, and contingency instructions;
- pre-session and post-session communications.

### 6. Participant package

Acceptance criteria:

- materials use consistent terminology and version identifiers;
- exercises can be completed without exposing confidential information;
- all files have semantic structure, readable typography, sufficient contrast, descriptive link text, and usable keyboard order;
- accessible alternatives exist for diagrams and any timed activity;
- participants receive preparation requirements, boundaries, materials, and follow-up instructions.

### 7. Bounded clinic translation

The clinic applies the method to one nonconfidential workflow per participant. It does not provide legal advice, product certification, procurement approval, or permission to process live sensitive data.

Acceptance criteria:

- eligibility, exclusions, capacity, intake, consent, conflict/sensitivity screen, deliverable, follow-up, cancellation, refund, and support rules are explicit;
- participants leave with a bounded decision record and next actions;
- cases outside scope receive a clear decline or referral path;
- clinic claims match actual staffing, time, and delivery capacity.

### 8. Commercial and communications journey

The full path must work: discover → understand → assess fit → register → pay → receive confirmation → prepare → attend → receive materials → follow up → optionally inquire about advisory work.

Acceptance criteria:

- price, date, timezone, capacity, delivery method, inclusions, exclusions, cancellation/refund terms, privacy notice, support contact, and accessibility request path are visible before purchase;
- payment, receipt, calendar, confirmation, reminder, cancellation, refund, and support flows pass end-to-end tests;
- no recurring subscription or monthly publication promise is implied unless separately approved;
- no affiliation, accreditation, CLE, certification, or outcome claim appears without verification and authorization.

### 9. Site integration and release QA

Acceptance criteria:

- Aloha AI, Decision Desk, Masterclass, Clinic, and Advisory have distinct roles and a coherent navigation path;
- Issue 01 is the flagship case; Issues 02–12 appear only as future or expansion work at their accurate readiness states;
- factual, legal, technical, editorial, visual, rights, accessibility, privacy, security, responsive, browser, email, payment, and support checks pass within recorded scopes;
- a complete rehearsal is documented;
- all known limitations and post-release monitoring owners are recorded;
- RN gives explicit release approval.

## Required visual package

The package must be evidentiary rather than decorative:

1. an original or rights-cleared photograph of a real intake environment containing no client information;
2. a legible, rights-reviewed primary-document image;
3. an original System X-Ray of the intake record, summarizer, reviewer, and downstream decisions;
4. screenshots only where they establish a specific product or workflow fact.

Every visual requires a purpose, factual caption, alt text, credit, source, date or capture date, rights basis, manipulation disclosure, and linked claim IDs. Generic AI imagery and staged screens containing real or plausibly identifying client data are prohibited.

## External dependencies and finite fallbacks

| Dependency | Required disposition | Finite fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor evidence or access | response deadline and evidence log | publish the no-product finding; do not fabricate testing |
| Documentary location or permission | written rights record | use another real, non-sensitive environment or omit the unsupported visual claim |
| Accessibility participants | scheduled review and findings | do not claim user validation; complete technical QA and label the human-testing gate open |
| Payment/email configuration | verified owner credentials and test transactions | keep enrollment inactive while the editorial and teaching release candidate remains reviewable |
| Independent legal review | named reviewer, scope, and disposition | narrow claims and label the review boundary; do not imply legal clearance |

An expired dependency must resolve to its fallback, a narrowed claim, or an explicit release blocker. It may not create unbounded research.

## Expansion firewall

The following are outside the flagship critical path unless a documented Issue 01 claim or operational dependency requires them:

- completing Issue 02 jurisdiction packets;
- researching the remaining 44 recording-law rows;
- producing Issues 03–12;
- proving exhaustive coverage of every jurisdiction, sector, firm type, or AI product;
- launching a recurring monthly subscription;
- authorizing live-data product use;
- building unrelated Aloha AI site features.

New work enters the critical path only through a change record stating the affected deliverable, evidence of necessity, added time, owner, and what existing scope is displaced.

## Release decision

Completion is binary at the system level: all nine deliverables pass, or the flagship remains unreleased. Individual assets may be marked complete without implying that the release unit is complete.

Current disposition: production authorized on the draft branch; publication, enrollment, payment activation, email sending, merge, deployment to production, live-data testing, and public release remain unauthorized pending RN approval.
