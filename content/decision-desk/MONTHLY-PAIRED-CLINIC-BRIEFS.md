# Decision Desk monthly paired clinic briefs

Status: governed clinic production briefs; not completed clinic packages, public offers, or release authorization  
Applies with: annual program contract, parity contract, commercial journey, and each issue completion contract  
Shared first-cohort assumption: four participants, two hours, one bounded nonconfidential workflow per participant, RN as substantive facilitator, and a named day-of support operator before rehearsal

## Shared clinic boundary

Every clinic applies one month-specific Decision Desk instrument. Participants may describe workflow structure, roles, decisions, source classes, and nonconfidential constraints. They may not submit names, raw records, protected communications, client/patient/student/employee/customer facts, credentials, production data, proprietary documents, or information they lack authority to share.

The output is a bounded decision record and next-action plan. It is not legal or professional advice, product certification, procurement approval, compliance clearance, or authorization to deploy AI or process live data.

Required shared states: eligible, clarification required, deferred, declined, capacity held, payment pending, confirmed, cancelled, refunded, attended, follow-up sent, correction open, closed, and incident hold.

## 01 — Improve Your Client Intake Workflow

- Application: one prospective-client intake workflow.
- Eligible participant: owner or authorized workflow lead able to discuss the process without case facts.
- Exclude/decline: active matter facts, prospective-client identity, privileged/confidential content, live product testing, jurisdiction-specific legal advice, or request for product approval.
- Clinic work: map authoritative intake record, conflict/urgency/routing decisions, proposed AI role, evidence gaps, human review, correction, and stop control.
- Deliverable: bounded Intake Summarization Evaluation Card and next-action record.
- Fictional clinic rehearsal: a small firm considering summary drafting after structured intake.
- Current state: substantially drafted under Issue 01; operational gates remain open.

## 02 — Plan a Safer AI-Assisted Meeting

- Application: one defined meeting class, not “all meetings.”
- Eligible participant: meeting owner, operations lead, counsel/compliance lead, or authorized policy owner who can identify participant roles and locations without naming people.
- Minimum intake: meeting purpose/class, participant role types, relevant locations/jurisdictions at a high level, attendance voluntariness, agenda transitions, current record, proposed bot functions, vendor/configuration if known, retention proposal, and objection path.
- Exclude/decline: covert capture, active disputes/investigations, union or works-council advice, privileged session facts, patient/student/client content, biometric identification, unknown participant locations that cannot be resolved, or a request for nationwide legal clearance.
- Clinic work: classify meeting segments; map authority/notice/permission layers; identify power and access barriers; trace audio/transcript/summary data; set bot entry/exit rules; separate transcript, summary, action items, and official record; design objection, pause, deletion, correction, and incident paths.
- Deliverable: Meeting Capture Permission and Record Map with allow/allow-with-controls/defer/reject disposition.
- Fictional clinic rehearsal: a distributed project meeting with employees, contractor, customer, accessibility request, confidential agenda transition, and late joiner.
- Critical stop conditions: unresolved applicable recording layer, non-optional participant with no safe alternative, bot cannot be removed for protected segment, unsafe retention/training, no authoritative record, or no correction/objection path.
- Clinic-specific evidence: federal interception baseline; bounded state/country recording rules; employment/labor and sector constraints; accessibility/language; vendor capture, training, retention, subprocessors, and deletion behavior.
- Required visual: meeting-boundary System X-Ray showing entry, notice, capture modes, record classes, exits, and deletion/correction.
- Current state: brief governed; full completion contract and clinic package next.

## 03 — Set Rules for AI Customer Support

- Application: one support-message class and its escalation path.
- Eligible participant: authorized support, operations, trust/safety, product, or compliance owner.
- Minimum intake: channel, message class, customer action/consequence, authentication, authoritative knowledge source, proposed AI function, human escalation, logs, correction, and rollback.
- Exclude/decline: individual customer disputes, account credentials, live tickets, regulated advice, emergency/safety response authorization, collections/legal strategy, or production-agent access.
- Clinic work: split classify/retrieve/draft/send/act permissions; map authentication and source authority; test complaint, fraud, accommodation, vulnerability, and safety escalation; set send/action thresholds and reversibility.
- Deliverable: Support Automation Authority Map and message-class disposition.
- Fictional clinic rehearsal: ordinary FAQ, billing dispute, accessibility request, safety complaint, and account-change request.
- Critical stop conditions: material complaint or harm not recognized, weak authentication, unsupported representation, inaccessible alternative, irreversible action, or no correction/escalation path.
- Required visual: message-class authority ladder and escalation handoff X-Ray.

## 04 — Make Internal AI Answers More Reliable

- Application: one internal question class and a bounded approved-source collection.
- Eligible participant: authorized knowledge, IT, operations, compliance, or internal-service owner.
- Minimum intake: user group, question class, source owners, permissions, update process, conflicting sources, answer consequence, citation/abstention behavior, feedback, and correction.
- Exclude/decline: uploading internal documents, privileged repositories, employee case facts, security secrets, credentials, universal “chat with everything,” or production retrieval testing.
- Clinic work: identify authoritative sources; map permission inheritance; resolve freshness/conflict rules; specify citation and abstention; create correction ownership and reliance boundaries.
- Deliverable: Knowledge Answerability and Source-Authority Test.
- Fictional clinic rehearsal: benefits policy, security procedure, client promise, and outdated wiki conflict.
- Critical stop conditions: no source owner, access mismatch, unresolved authority conflict, missing citations, stale-content risk without monitoring, or no correction path.
- Required visual: source-authority and permission flow X-Ray.

## 05 — Review AI in Hiring and Selection

- Application: one stage of one opportunity workflow.
- Eligible participant: authorized owner of hiring, fellowship, admissions, grant, accelerator, creator, or procurement-opportunity process.
- Minimum intake: opportunity/stage, stakes, criteria, features/proxies, decision maker, affected groups, accommodations, notice, records, contest path, and proposed AI role.
- Exclude/decline: applicant records, protected-trait data, active selection decisions, individualized employment/legal advice, validation claims without studies, or live ranking.
- Clinic work: distinguish eligibility, screening, scoring, ranking, and recommendation; map criteria to job/program purpose; identify proxies and missingness; assess accommodations, distributional evidence, notice, contest, and human reliance.
- Deliverable: Human Opportunity Decision Impact Map with prohibit/redesign/evaluate/bounded-pilot disposition.
- Fictional clinic rehearsal: fellowship shortlist with nonlinear experience, disability accommodation, missing data, and proxy features.
- Critical stop conditions: invalid criteria, unmeasured material subgroup harm, inaccessible participation, illusory appeal, or consequential automated rejection.
- Required visual: opportunity-stage consequence and contestability X-Ray.

## 06 — Check AI-Written Public Claims

- Application: one nonconfidential public-claim class and approval workflow.
- Eligible participant: authorized marketing, communications, product, scientific/regulatory, legal, or founder-side claim owner.
- Minimum intake: channel, claim class, intended audience, evidence owner, approver, source types, versioning, endorsements/comparisons, correction, and withdrawal.
- Exclude/decline: unreleased campaign copy, trade secrets, patient/customer data, active regulatory response, legal opinion, individualized claim clearance, or request to generate/publish copy.
- Clinic work: classify objective/subjective/comparative/endorsement/scientific/sustainability claims; separate source fact from inference; map substantiation and approval; set version/channel controls and withdrawal.
- Deliverable: Public-Claim Substantiation Gate and evidence-gap plan.
- Fictional clinic rehearsal: launch claims about performance, sustainability, expert support, customer outcomes, and competitors.
- Critical stop conditions: missing competent evidence, unauthorized endorsement, misleading comparison, unverifiable inference, or no approval/correction record.
- Required visual: source-to-claim-to-approval lineage X-Ray.

## 07 — Make High-Stakes AI Explanations Clear

- Application: one authoritative notice or explanation class, never individual advice.
- Eligible participant: authorized health, legal-information, financial-information, benefits, safety, or public-service content owner.
- Minimum intake: authoritative source, audience, action consequence, exceptions, uncertainty, reading/language needs, disability access, escalation, correction, and proposed AI role.
- Exclude/decline: personal eligibility or diagnosis, legal advice, financial recommendation, emergency triage, individual records, or live translation of protected facts.
- Clinic work: establish authoritative meaning; identify material exceptions and uncertainty; test fidelity and comprehension; create language/access alternatives; define advice boundary and escalation.
- Deliverable: High-Stakes Explanation Fidelity Test and controlled explanation specification.
- Fictional clinic rehearsal: benefits and safety notice with eligibility ambiguity, language variants, and accessibility needs.
- Critical stop conditions: meaning drift, omitted exception, false certainty, inaccessible output, or advice-like personalization.
- Required visual: authority-to-explanation fidelity and escalation X-Ray.

## 08 — Plan Synthetic Media With Permission

- Application: one proposed, rights-cleared synthetic-media use.
- Eligible participant: authorized media, archive, museum, education, entertainment, or communications owner able to discuss the concept without uploading protected assets.
- Minimum intake: represented person/type, living/deceased status, voice/likeness elements, purpose/audience, rights holders, labor/cultural context, disclosure, provenance, security, withdrawal, and correction.
- Exclude/decline: nonconsensual intimate content, impersonation/fraud, political persuasion, active-rights dispute, cloning a participant during clinic, unidentified rights holder, or request for legal clearance.
- Clinic work: map identity and source assets; scope permission; distinguish restoration, reenactment, simulation, and evidence; set provenance/disclosure; assess labor/cultural authority; design revocation and removal.
- Deliverable: Synthetic Media Permission and Provenance Record.
- Fictional clinic rehearsal: museum short involving deceased historical figure, living narrator, synthetic restoration, and multilingual distribution.
- Critical stop conditions: absent authority, misleading documentary effect, unmanageable revocation, insecure model/asset handling, or material cultural harm.
- Required visual: person/asset/permission/provenance/withdrawal X-Ray.

## 09 — Set Safe Limits for AI Agents

- Application: one nonproduction action class using synthetic records only.
- Eligible participant: authorized operations, finance, IT, security, product, or compliance workflow owner.
- Minimum intake: principal, proposed action, target system class, consequence, credentials/permissions, approval threshold, idempotency, logs, reconciliation, rollback, incident owner, and vendor/configuration.
- Exclude/decline: credentials, API keys, production access, live transactions, security testing, irreversible action authorization, financial/legal advice, or live customer/employee records.
- Clinic work: distinguish propose/prepare/execute; map principal and authority; minimize privilege; test duplication, partial failure, stale state, rollback, and human approval; define monitoring and kill switch.
- Deliverable: Agent Action Authority and Reversibility Matrix.
- Fictional clinic rehearsal: draft email, CRM update, calendar event, refund proposal, and account-status change.
- Critical stop conditions: ambiguous authority, excess credentials, irreversibility, no idempotency/reconciliation, or nonfunctional stop control.
- Required visual: action authority, system boundary, confirmation, and rollback X-Ray.

## 10 — Review an AI Vendor Before You Buy

- Application: one nonconfidential acquisition/use proposal.
- Eligible participant: authorized executive, procurement, IT/security, privacy, operations, implementation, or counsel-side owner.
- Minimum intake: bounded use, data classes, exact product/configuration, available public or shareable evidence, proposed terms categories, service dependencies, change/exit needs, and decision deadline.
- Exclude/decline: confidential contracts, privileged negotiation strategy, regulated legal opinion, procurement certification, security penetration testing, or permission to sign.
- Clinic work: reconcile sales claims, product settings, DPA/security evidence, subprocessors, training/retention, IP/confidentiality, service/change terms, incident notice, portability, deletion, and exit.
- Deliverable: AI Vendor Evidence and Contract Dossier with accept-for-further-diligence/renegotiate/defer/reject disposition.
- Fictional clinic rehearsal: conflicting sales deck, DPA, security page, order form, and product settings.
- Critical stop conditions: material evidence gap, unacceptable reuse, unilateral change, weak incident/exit rights, or configuration mismatch.
- Required visual: vendor claim-to-contract-to-configuration evidence X-Ray.

## 11 — Set Rules for AI in Teaching and Assessment

- Application: one bounded instructional stage using fictional learner records.
- Eligible participant: authorized educator, program owner, instructional designer, assessment owner, accessibility/student-support lead, or procurement owner.
- Minimum intake: learner population, instructional stage, construct, consequence, proposed AI role, evidence of validity, authorship assumptions, accommodations, privacy, notice, educator review, contest, and records.
- Exclude/decline: real student work, minors’ data, disability records, active discipline/grade disputes, plagiarism adjudication, individualized education/legal advice, or live scoring.
- Clinic work: separate formative feedback from judgment; define construct and evidence; examine surveillance/authorship assumptions; map development, bias, access, accommodation, notice, contest, and educator responsibility.
- Deliverable: Learner-Affecting AI Use Review.
- Fictional clinic rehearsal: adult professional course with formative feedback, suspected AI use, multilingual learner, and accommodation request.
- Critical stop conditions: invalid inference, inaccessible process, excessive surveillance, unreviewable consequence, or displaced educator accountability.
- Required visual: learning-stage consequence, evidence, educator review, and contest X-Ray.

## 12 — Decide Whether to Keep, Change, or Stop an AI System

- Application: one fictionalized or nonconfidential continuation/retirement decision.
- Eligible participant: authorized executive, system owner, audit/risk, operations, procurement, compliance, or affected-person advocate.
- Minimum intake: original authorization, current use/configuration, monitoring, incidents/complaints, affected groups, workarounds, vendor/contract state, rollback/exit, data return/deletion, replacement, continuity, notice, and redress.
- Exclude/decline: undisclosed incidents, identifiable complainants, privileged investigations, active litigation facts, live shutdown authority, or request for compliance clearance.
- Clinic work: reconstruct baseline; identify scope/configuration/vendor drift; assess performance, incidents, unequal effects, hidden labor, and unsupported benefit; test rollback, replacement, deletion, notice, and redress.
- Deliverable: AI Continuation and Retirement Decision Record with continue/constrain/pause/rollback/replace/retire disposition.
- Fictional clinic rehearsal: a once-bounded system changed by vendor updates, workflow expansion, unequal errors, and human workarounds.
- Critical stop conditions: missing monitoring, material drift, unresolved harm, nonfunctional stop path, unavailable data exit, or unsupported benefit.
- Required visual: authorization-to-drift-to-stop/exit lifecycle X-Ray.
