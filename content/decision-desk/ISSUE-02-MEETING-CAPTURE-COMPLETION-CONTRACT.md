# Issue 02 — Meeting Capture completion contract

Status: active production contract; draft branch only
Public title: **Before the Bot Joins the Meeting**
Paired clinic: **Meeting Capture Decision Desk Clinic**
Disposition: production authorized; publication, enrollment, payment, sending, human capture, merge, and release unauthorized

## Governed decision

For one precisely defined meeting class, should an organization prohibit capture, continue with human notes only, redesign and test a synthetic configuration, or seek separate approval for a bounded human pilot?

The issue does not answer whether a vendor, bot category, organization, or jurisdiction is universally “compliant.” It does not authorize recording people. A headquarters address, calendar notice, bot icon, beep, or participant silence is not treated as universal permission.

## Completion rule

Issue 02 is complete only when all nine layers independently satisfy the parity contract. Shared Issue 01 templates may establish form, but copied substance cannot satisfy an Issue 02 criterion.

### 1. Research and evidence system

The actual meeting packet identifies meeting class, segments, participant roles and locations, voluntariness, sector, organization/worker status, purpose, record owner, uses, access, retention, exact configuration, storage/support locations, and later disclosure. Every material claim maps to a current source and pinpoint. Recording/interception, labor, privilege/confidentiality, records/discovery, privacy/sector, accessibility/language, and cross-border layers are separately evaluated. Conflicts, counterevidence, negative searches, uncertainty, and freshness triggers are preserved. Zero jurisdictions may be called cleared until every implicated layer and qualified review requirement passes.

### 2. Article and Source Desk

The article distinguishes raw media, transcript, generated summary, extracted actions, and human-approved authoritative record. It distinguishes controlling authority, official guidance, vendor statement, observed result, stakeholder evidence, RN analysis, recommendation, and unknown. The article, Source Desk, Permission and Record Map, teaching materials, clinic, and visitor journey agree. Public claims remain bounded to actual evidence.

### 3. Meeting Capture Permission and Record Map

The instrument records purpose, class/segments, people and power, authority layers, sensitivity, exact capture modes, five record classes, vendor flow, access, retention, correction, objection/pause/exit, incident path, reviewers, decision date, and disposition.

Allowed outputs: do not capture; human notes only; redesign and synthetic test; or bounded pilot for separate approval. It never outputs legal clearance.

### 4. Product and behavioral evidence

One exact configuration may be evaluated only with freezeable public evidence and synthetic data. No live, personal, privileged, confidential, employee, client, patient, student, customer, or community-participant data is used. Accuracy tests include omission, insertion, speaker attribution, action ownership, dissent, uncertainty, language/accent, interruption, audio quality, and sensitive-segment transition. Behavioral effects remain unclaimed unless actually studied. Vendor silence resolves to a no-product finding.

### 5. Ninety-minute masterclass

Participants classify one fictional distributed meeting, map authority and power, separate records, apply the Permission and Record Map, identify evidence gaps, design objection/pause/correction/incident paths, and reach a bounded disposition.

Required assets: semantic slide deck, workbook, fictional case, completed example, answer key, facilitator guide, preparation guide, communications, resource guide, feedback/correction instrument, accessible-delivery manifest, and rehearsal protocol.

### 6. Paired clinic

One authorized participant applies the method to one nonconfidential meeting class. Required six-record core: operating specification; minimal intake and screen; acknowledgments and no-recording protocol; decision-record template and fictional example; commercial/support rules; and follow-up/correction/privacy/incident/decline protocol. The clinic provides a bounded decision record, not legal advice, certification, procurement approval, compliance clearance, or deployment authorization.

### 7. Commercial and visitor journey

Offer roles, fit screen, human clinic screening, consent, price, date, timezone, capacity, delivery, terms, privacy, accessibility, payment, receipt, calendar, reminders, cancellation, refund, support, follow-up, and advisory inquiry must be specified and tested. All 24+ month-specific scenarios must pass before activation.

### 8. Visual and accessible delivery package

Required: original meeting-boundary System X-Ray; rights-reviewed primary-document image; real permitted environment photograph or explicit omission; captions; alt text; credits; sources; dates; rights bases; manipulation disclosures; and claim mappings. Generic bot imagery is prohibited. Canonical semantic HTML and plain text are required. Other formats may be called complete only after file-level accessibility and rendered QA.

### 9. Readiness, rehearsals, and approval

Research reconciliation, legal-review disposition, editorial QA, technical accessibility QA, rendered observation, commercial scenarios, masterclass rehearsal, clinic rehearsal, defect remediation, named operational owners, and RN approval are separately recorded.

## Presumptive no-capture boundary

Absent a separately completed packet, do not capture privileged/legal strategy, personnel discipline or performance, protected labor activity, health/care, security incident, safety report, sensitive community/listening, relationship repair, accommodation disclosure, active dispute/investigation, or any segment where meaningful refusal or an equivalent non-capture path is unavailable.

## Finite fallbacks

- Incomplete authority packet → human notes only or do not capture.
- Unfreezeable product evidence → no-product finding.
- No permitted photograph → omit it and narrow the visual claim.
- No independent legal reviewer → narrow claims and disclose no legal clearance.
- No human accessibility participants → complete technical QA and leave human validation open.
- No configured/tested providers → keep registration inactive.
- Failed or unavailable rehearsal → no release.

## Current state

The authority method, 51-jurisdiction fail-closed inventory, seven partial recording layers, meeting taxonomy, permission-record map, synthetic benchmark, configuration dossier, evaluation protocol, and R1 article exist. Zero full jurisdiction packets are cleared. This contract activates the remaining full-parity work without changing that evidence boundary.
