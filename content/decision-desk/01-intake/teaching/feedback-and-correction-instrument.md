# Decision Desk 01 — feedback and correction instrument

Version 1.0 · data-minimizing draft

## Collection rule

Collect only what is needed to improve the material, investigate a correction, or respond to an access barrier. Do not request employer, client, matter, health, disability, or other sensitive details. Free-text fields must repeat: “Do not include confidential, privileged, client, employer-sensitive, or identifying personal information.”

Anonymous submission should be available unless a response is requested.

## Internal triage

| Severity | Definition | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Could reverse the disposition, expose sensitive data, misstate authorization, or prevent essential access | Withdraw/disable affected asset; notify owner immediately; do not distribute until disposition |
| High | Material claim, source, boundary, or access pathway is wrong or unusable | Acknowledge; assign owner; correct before next delivery |
| Medium | Localized error with a usable workaround | Record, schedule correction, disclose workaround |
| Low | Style, clarity, or nonmaterial enhancement | Backlog with disposition |

## Privacy and retention gate

Before activation, approve where responses are stored, who may access them, retention/deletion period, response-time promise, export/deletion procedure, and incident contact. Do not connect feedback to marketing without separate, specific consent.
