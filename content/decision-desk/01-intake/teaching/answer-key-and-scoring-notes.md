# Decision Desk 01 — facilitator answer key and scoring notes

Version 1.0 · do not distribute before the exercise

## Core answer

The defensible case disposition is `human_led_only`. Gate 1 fails; Gates 2–4 are unresolved; Gate 5 was not run. The firm may redesign its workflow. It may not nominate a candidate on this record, begin synthetic testing, process live data, or begin a pilot.

Participants need not reproduce model wording. Score the quality and boundaries of the reasoning.

## Rubric (20 points)

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision framing | Chooses “AI” generally | Names summarization but not owner/scope | Exact use, owner, workflow step, affected people |
| Authority levels | Treats all sources alike | Notes jurisdiction variation | Separates controlling/model/advisory/vendor/empirical/analysis |
| Source-of-truth design | Synopsis replaces source | Says “human review” | Preserves original, structured fields, provenance, decisions |
| Gate 1 | Passes/ignores | Calls unresolved | Fails with workflow/baseline/owner evidence |
| Gate 2 | Selects brand | Unresolved without reason | Unresolved; lists freezeable configuration gaps |
| Gate 3 | Assumes compliance | Generic privacy concern | Names authority/data/contract/access gaps; prohibits live data |
| Gate 4 | Treats benchmark as universal | Accepts set without firm validation | Requires intended-use mapping, reviewer approval, thresholds |
| I01-002 fidelity | Misses critical errors | Finds some errors | Finds client/causation/role/date/evidence certainty and missing letter |
| Human factors | “Human in loop” suffices | Mentions source check | Requires observed source review, seeded corrections, stop evidence |
| Disposition | Pilot/product approval | Vague delay | Human-led plus finite redesign, owners, reopening and stop authority |

Suggested interpretation: 17–20 ready to produce a bounded record; 13–16 understands method but needs review; 9–12 material boundary errors; 0–8 revisit the case before transfer.

The score is instructional, not certification or proof of competence.

## Critical misconceptions

Pause and correct immediately if a participant:

- treats Noel as a client;
- states that September 3 is a verified legal deadline;
- treats vendor security/privacy copy as proof of the exact configuration;
- claims the 51-jurisdiction baseline clears a deployment;
- assumes a human reviewer catches errors without observed evidence;
- recommends live data because the benchmark is fictional;
- interprets `human_led_only` as a universal prohibition on AI.

## Acceptable variation

A participant may choose “reject” instead of `human_led_only` if they clearly reject the present proposal while preserving the possibility of a newly scoped future proposal. “Redesign and retest” is incomplete unless they explain that testing itself is not yet authorized because Gates 2–4 remain unresolved.

## I01-002 required observations

Minimum passing response:

- exact September 3 statement preserved with attribution;
- missing letter preserved;
- urgency elevated without declaring legal effect;
- “client,” causation, driver identity, police documentation, and evidentiary value removed;
- representation status remains open;
- immediate letter request, attorney review, jurisdiction facts, and conflict entities requested.

## Debrief prompts

1. Which failure existed before any AI tool appeared?
2. Which missing fact had the greatest routing consequence?
3. Why is “human review” a hypothesis rather than a control result?
4. What evidence would permit synthetic testing but still not a live pilot?
5. What change should automatically reopen the record?
