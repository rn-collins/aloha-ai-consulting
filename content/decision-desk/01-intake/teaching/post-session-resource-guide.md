## The method in seven moves

1. State the exact decision, owner, workflow step, affected people, and evidenced problem.
2. Map controlling authority and distinguish it from model rules, guidance, vendor statements, observations, analysis, and unknowns.
3. Redesign the workflow and preserve the authoritative source record.
4. Name and freeze an exact product/configuration—or record that no candidate qualifies.
5. Approve a representative fictional test set and predetermined thresholds before testing.
6. Observe human behavior and whole-process outcomes, not merely model output.
7. Record reject, redesign and retest, continue human-led, or recommend a separately governed bounded pilot.

## Corrections and updates

A correction report should identify the asset, version, section, issue, supporting source if available, and access impact. Do not include sensitive personal or matter information. Aloha AI should acknowledge receipt, triage severity, record disposition, and publish material corrections with a date and explanation.

## What this package does not do

It does not create an attorney-client, consulting, or advisory relationship; provide jurisdiction-specific legal advice; certify a participant; approve procurement; endorse a vendor; or authorize synthetic testing, live data, or a pilot.

Any clinic or advisory inquiry is separately screened, scoped, accepted, and documented.
