## Reopening packet

A candidate becomes eligible for synthetic evaluation only after the owner obtains and freezes:

1. exact contracting entity, product, feature, tier, tenant type, and price/proposal date;
2. a demonstration using only the frozen synthetic development split, showing the original source beside the synopsis;
3. model/configuration visibility, system instructions or equivalent, output schema, retry behavior, and material-change notice;
4. complete intake-to-output data-flow and subprocessor diagram;
5. executed or reviewable terms, DPA, security schedule, retention/deletion schedule, training-use terms, incident notice, data-location terms, and exit rights;
6. role/permission matrix, audit/export capability, deletion test, accessibility record, and supported-language record;
7. ability to preserve the original submission, link every synopsis to its source and configuration, prevent automatic downstream action, and require human source review; and
8. named firm owner, qualified privacy/security/legal reviewers, stop authority, and prohibited-use policy.

If a candidate cannot disclose or freeze a field, record it as vendor-controlled and unobservable. Any unresolved high-impact field keeps the candidate ineligible for live data and may make it ineligible even for a meaningful synthetic comparison.
