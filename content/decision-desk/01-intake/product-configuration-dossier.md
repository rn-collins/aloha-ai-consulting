# Product and configuration dossier

Status: governed pre-evaluation record; bounded shortlist reviewed; no product eligible or selected; no live-data use authorized.

## Purpose

This dossier prevents a category claim such as “AI intake summarization works” from substituting for evidence about the exact system a firm would deploy. Complete and freeze one dossier for every materially different product, model, configuration, integration, matter class, or data pathway.

## Minimum reproducibility packet

Preserve the system prompt or equivalent instructions, available generation settings, structured-output schema, preprocessing and redaction, source-to-output mapping, retry behavior, logging and retention settings, reviewer interface, and exact test-set version. If a vendor does not expose a field, mark it “vendor-controlled and unobservable”; do not infer a value.

## Material-change rule

Reopen review and rerun affected tests when any model, prompt, account tier, integration, retention term, training-use term, subprocessor, data location, reviewer interface, matter class, or output schema changes. Silent vendor change is itself a stop condition until assessed.
