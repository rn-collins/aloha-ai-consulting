# Issue 01 real-environment shot and permission protocol

Status: pending real reporting; no photograph claimed  
Owner: RN Collins or named commissioned photographer

## Required establishing photograph

Capture one real small-firm intake workspace in ordinary working condition. The frame should show the relationship among the desk, intake device, note-taking or routing materials, and the human review position without showing any client, visitor, case name, calendar entry, notification, file label, badge, address, or confidential information.

## Before capture

1. Obtain written permission from the firm/property representative and every recognizable participant.
2. Confirm whether the firm name and location may be disclosed, generalized, or withheld.
3. Close all live systems; clear desks of papers and labels; disable notifications.
4. Use a blank screen or a clearly marked fictional training record prepared before the shoot.
5. Photograph the cleared scene once, then inspect every frame at full resolution for reflections and background disclosures.
6. Record photographer, date, location treatment, equipment, permissions, edits, and file hash.

## Preferred frame

- Horizontal 3:2 or 4:3 establishing frame, with a vertical alternate if feasible.
- Eye-level or slightly elevated perspective; no artificial “hacker” lighting.
- Human presence may be shown through hands or an unidentifiable seated posture only when consent is explicit.
- Do not stage a person as a client.
- Do not show a product interface unless that exact configuration has separately passed the evidence gate.

## Caption template

“An intake workspace at [named or generalized location], photographed [date]. All screens were blank or used clearly fictional material; no client information appears. The image documents the working environment, not the performance of an AI system.”

## Alt-text template

“[Concise description of the desk, device, review materials, and human position]. All screens are blank or display clearly fictional training information.”

## Finite disposition

Permission deadline: owner-selected production date, recorded before release review. If permission or a safe location is unavailable, mark V003 omitted-permission and remove any claim that the released article contains original environmental reporting. Stock, generated, or borrowed imagery is not an allowed fallback.
