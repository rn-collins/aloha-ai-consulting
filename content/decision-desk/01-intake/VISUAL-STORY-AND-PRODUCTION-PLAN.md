## Editorial rule

Every visual must establish a fact, method, environment, or evidence boundary. Generic AI imagery, stock “law office” imagery, generated photographs, decorative dashboards, and screens containing real or plausibly identifying client information are prohibited.

## Manipulation standard

For original photographs, permitted edits are exposure, white balance, straightening, restrained crop, and removal of embedded metadata. No object removal, compositing, generative fill, relighting that changes the scene, or replacement of screen content. A blank or visibly fictional screen must be arranged before capture, not inserted afterward.

For documentary graphics, typography and layout are editorial reconstructions. They must never be described as screenshots or facsimiles. Source identity, date, limitations, and the route to the complete authority remain visible.

## Release gate

The visual package is not complete until each published asset has a final file, factual caption, alt text or adjacent long description, credit, source, capture/creation date, rights basis, manipulation disclosure, linked claim IDs, sensitivity review, and responsive/accessibility QA. If the real photograph cannot be lawfully obtained by the production deadline, omit it and narrow the article’s photojournalistic claim; do not substitute stock or generated photography.
