# Research question

Which customer-support functions, message classes, and authority levels—if any—may a small business responsibly assign to AI, under what sector, consumer-protection, privacy, accessibility, discrimination, safety, security, and operational controls?

## Required research modules

1. Separate classification, retrieval, drafting, communication, and account/financial action.
2. FTC Act and applicable sector/state consumer-protection requirements.
3. Privacy, authentication, security, records, data minimization, and vendor lifecycle.
4. Accessibility, language, disability, discrimination, vulnerable-customer, and escalation evidence.
5. Product architecture: retrieval source, model, tools/actions, permissions, reversibility, logs, and versioning.
6. Incidents, complaints, enforcement matters, audits, and empirical evaluations.
7. Measurement: material claims, escalation recall, first-contact resolution, repeat contacts, corrections, complaints, abandonment, and unequal failure.
8. Human escalation design and the cost of forcing customers to repeat the problem.

## Disconfirmation question

Which harms, authority failures, or evidence gaps require the organization to keep the category fully human-led?
