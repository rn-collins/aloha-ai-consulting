# Aloha AI Masterclass Working Guide

## What Should We Actually Use AI For?

This guide accompanies Aloha AI's free, self-paced masterclass. It supports general education and reflection. It is not legal, medical, financial, security, procurement, accessibility, or other regulated professional advice. Do not enter confidential, privileged, personal, regulated, credential, payment, or security-sensitive information.

### 1. Name the decision—not the technology

- What decision would still exist if every AI product disappeared tomorrow?
- What outcome matters, for whom, and compared with what current result?
- Who owns the decision and its consequences?

### 3. Keep four paths open

Record the strongest case for each: repair or simplify the current process; keep the work human-led; investigate and gather evidence; use no new intervention now; or test a narrowly bounded AI-assisted step.

### 4. Test the operating conditions

For each item, record present / missing / uncertain and the evidence: authority and owner; appropriate data and permission; affected-party input; accessibility; privacy and security; human review; measurement; incident response; stop conditions; and fallback.

### 5. Choose the smallest honest next step

Choose one: keep human-led; repair the process; gather evidence; compare options; or design a bounded, reversible pilot.
