Fit and boundaries

The strongest fit is work too complex for a template and recurring enough to deserve a system.

Aloha AI is a fit when

  • Information is fragmented or difficult to verify.
  • Rules, exceptions, and professional judgment matter.
  • The team repeats the same research, review, intake, or decision work.
  • A service or methodology could become a reusable product.
  • A working pilot is needed before a larger software investment.

A different partner may be needed when

  • The requirement is primarily commodity web design or standard software configuration.
  • The organization needs large-scale engineering staff augmentation.
  • The requested output requires legal, medical, tax, security, clinical, or other licensed professional judgment that has not been retained.
  • No accountable internal owner can participate in decisions and review.
  • The goal is unsupervised automation of consequential work.
