01The cost of scaling content you have not grounded+

You already know AI can write a week of posts in an afternoon. In a regulated shop, every one of those posts is a potential compliance artifact: logged, supervised, and read back to you later by someone whose job is to find the sentence that should not have been published.

A single ungrounded claim is a correction, a finding, or a fine. Automate the generation of that claim without grounding it and you have not removed the risk. You have multiplied it by your posting volume. Every channel. Every week. On a schedule.

Two costs compound at once. Hallucination risk: models reliably produce statements no source can verify, and it scales with your automation. Brand risk: ungrounded content fails the reader's source-credibility check and off-voice content fails the fluency check, because inconsistent writing is judged as less true. Faster is not the goal. Trusted-and-fast is.

02What's inside+
  • The Five-Domain Knowledge Base. The exact folder structure, file conventions, and provenance rules that turn verified facts into a retrieval surface your model writes from.
  • All 20 prompts, written out in full. Each built from a named prompt-engineering technique (role prompting, chain-of-thought, few-shot exemplars, XML structuring, grounding) and annotated so you see the mechanism.
  • A fill-in Voice Configuration template. Brand voice pinned to the Nielsen Norman Group's four measurable dimensions of tone, so "make it sound like us" becomes a target the model can hit.
  • A verified pricing table for the platform-intelligence stack: real prices checked live, with anything unconfirmed marked Unverified rather than guessed.
  • A setup checklist, a provenance rule set, and clear when-to-hire criteria, plus the honest caveats most guides skip.
03Who it's for+
  • Compliance-minded teams in healthcare, financial services, insurance, legal, and government who want to use AI for content and cannot afford a confident, fluent, wrong sentence to reach the public.
  • Teams with the time and internal ownership to build the system themselves. This is your complete specification.
  • Teams who do not: it tells you precisely when to commission the full build instead.
