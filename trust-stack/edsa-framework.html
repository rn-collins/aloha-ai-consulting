01The gap most regulated creators never see+

The moment you publish a video about psilocybin, cannabis, or a prescription drug, two things appraise it. The first is a human nervous system, running a fast pre-deliberative check: expert or amateur, honest or selling, safe or a threat. If that appraisal returns "threat," nothing you say afterward lands.

The second is a classifier. A recommendation system runs its own threat appraisal: safe to circulate, or near a policy line. If it returns "threat," your video is quietly withheld from the feed. Not removed, just never shown. No audience. No appeal. No notification.

Most regulated creators optimize for one judge and lose to the other. Content the brain distrusts does not convert. Content the classifier reads as a threat does not reach anyone. You can be compliant and invisible, or compelling and suppressed. The trap is thinking those are your only two options.

02What's inside+
  • The dual-appraisal crosswalk, the analytical core: every element mapped to its cited brain mechanism and its cited platform threat.
  • The four ordered elements. Evidence, Discussion, Self-Determination, Action, each lowering the brain's threat appraisal and defusing a specific platform-policy trigger.
  • Three worked before/after rewrites (psilocybin, cannabis, prescription antidepressants) showing the architecture defusing both appraisals at once.
  • An annotated 0:00–12:00 script template, beat by beat, naming the mechanism and the platform payoff for each.
  • A one-page quick-reference card, the five hard "never"s, dated, paraphrased platform-policy summaries with source URLs, and full references.
03Who it's for+
  • Producers, agencies, and in-house content teams building long- or short-form video for legal cannabis operators, state-licensed psilocybin services, and psychedelic-adjacent education.
  • Biopharma and mental-health communication teams that must survive automated review while staying truthful.
  • Anyone who wants to understand, at the level of mechanism, why the compliant version is also the version that converts.
