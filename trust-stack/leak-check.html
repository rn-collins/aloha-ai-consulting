Before you rely on it

A six-layer self-assessment for regulated operators · Aloha AI · RN Collins

Trust is a nervous-system event. Platforms, audiences, regulators, and buyers all run the same loop: appraise the source, scan for threat, grant or deny. This tool scores your operation on all six layers of the Regulated Trust Stack, then returns your weakest layer, the layers above it inheriting the damage, and where to start.

Answer for what is true today, not what you intend. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; scoring runs entirely in your browser.
